# IA Strategy and Deliverables

Turn architecture work into actionable strategy and deliverables: test candidate structures before
commitment, pair strategy with a plan, and tailor sitemaps, wireframes, diagrams, and guidance to
audience and purpose over polish.

## When this applies

- Communicating IA strategy to stakeholders, clients, colleagues, or implementation teams (P021).
- Cross-functional teams are resolving interface, navigation, architecture, and implementation questions (P031).
- Preparing architecture documentation for designers, developers, editors, or production staff (P032).
- Moving from strategy into design or implementation (P045).
- Creating or presenting IA diagrams or nonvisual equivalents (P046).
- Exploring or communicating broad architecture and navigation concepts (P047).
- Creating multiple wireframes or a wireframe set for clients or production teams (P048).
- writing a practitioner-facing design guide (P049).
- synthesizing evidence for a practice (P061).
- Designing important, complex, unique, confusing, or template-setting pages and screens (P066).

## Procedure

Apply these principles to the situation under review; for each, name the user need at stake and the trade-off the choice carries.

1. Use scenarios, stories, case studies, diagrams, sitemaps, wireframes, and reports to make abstract IA strategy understandable and actionable for different audiences. (P021)
2. Use sketches, wireframes, and prototypes to integrate IA with visual design, technical feasibility, and team vocabulary before committing to code or finished graphics. (P031)
3. Make implementation-stage sitemaps simple, modular, traceable, and production-ready with condensed notation, unique IDs, hierarchy coverage, labels, and navigation specifications. (P032)
4. Pair IA strategy with a project plan and presentations so recommendations become feasible, sequenced, integrated with other teams, and understood by stakeholders. (P045)
5. Treat IA diagrams as partial communication views; tailor each view to audience and need, present it live when possible, and value usefulness over polish. (P046)
6. Use high-level sitemaps as purpose-specific, selective discussion tools for exploring organization, navigation, ownership, management, and access paths. (P047)
7. Choose wireframe fidelity and production practices deliberately, maintaining consistency, reusable templates, callouts, revision metadata, and team stewardship. (P048)
8. Deliver guidance in simple, objective, and clear language with short explanations, concrete per-practice examples, extended before-and-after examples, and references to supporting studies; state the objective and scope or limitations early and do not present it as exhaustive or as a full technical or process manual. (P049)
9. Base conversational guidance on primary, full research studies that measure user-centered outcomes such as satisfaction and trust, excluding studies that report no user impact or only algorithmic or technical performance, and passing each study through a quality checklist. (P061)
10. Use wireframes to connect IA with interaction design by clarifying page structure, visible components, grouping, priority, responsive behavior, and navigation overload. (P066)
