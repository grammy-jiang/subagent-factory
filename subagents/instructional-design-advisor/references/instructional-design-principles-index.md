# Instructional-Design Principles Index

Package-wide index of every promoted principle, grouped by the skill that owns it. Each entry restates the principle's operative core; the full statement lives in `../principles/principles.yaml`.

## Backward Design And Constructive Alignment

Skill: `backward-design-and-constructive-alignment`

- **P003** — Keep the unit as the design focus.
- **P008** — Check alignment with a three-column worksheet.
- **P009** — Ensure students understand what the learning eventually requires them to do.
- **P013** — Understand backward design as called backward only because many teachers begin with the means, and follow its order.
- **P014** — Sort content into three nested priority tiers.
- **P020** — Answer the coverage problem by identifying the fundamental knowledge of the subject.
- **P046** — Reject covering the content as a learning goal.
- **P055** — Expect the design process to be always iterative, with designers revisiting what they are after, how to assess it, and how to teach it, and rethinking earlier units in light of later designs.
- **P064** — Adapt constructive alignment to local context while preserving its core relationship.
- **P088** — Derive course and programme outcomes from graduate purposes, embedding both foundational and higher-order capabilities in authentic disciplinary or professional contexts.
- **P101** — Develop complex graduate capabilities through disciplinary practice, repeated programme experiences, institutional ethos, and integrated evidence.
- **P111** — When an activity stands in for learning, state the intended result and success criteria, connect them repeatedly to the activity, and show representative evidence.
- **P125** — Keep programme and course outcomes few, integrated, relevant, and manageable; each must state what learners will newly do and how well, while grouping compatible topics under common performances.
- **P135** — Prioritise content deliberately.
- **P172** — Implement alignment as one agenda.

## Learning Outcomes And Taxonomy

Skill: `learning-outcomes-and-taxonomy`

- **P001** — Use taxonomy as an adaptable analytic heuristic, not a literal reality, source of educational aims, guaranteed solution, or unique teaching prescription.
- **P031** — Use the objective to set up the assessment situation directly.
- **P076** — Set stage-appropriate levels of understanding and use SOLO to distinguish relevant detail from integration, coherent relation, and transfer to broader domains.
- **P077** — Resolve broad objective language into the specific knowledge subtype and cognitive process that successful learner performance actually requires.
- **P105** — Use analyze, evaluate, and create either as explicit transfer objectives or as means to simpler learning, and allow them to initiate or iterate with other processes rather than imposing a fixed.
- **P115** — Word objectives in terms of intended cognition rather than observable response format.
- **P141** — Distinguish routine execution from adaptive implementation; for the latter, require recognition of the problem type, selection or construction of a fitting method, adaptation, and execution.
- **P142** — Use analysis to select relevant elements, organize their relation to the whole, and infer underlying purpose; represent the resulting structure explicitly when useful.
- **P153** — Distinguish remembering from understanding in every objective and every test.
- **P161** — To assess transfer or cognition beyond remembering, use sufficiently novel material to prevent memorized reproduction without adding irrelevant knowledge demands.
- **P170** — Build and assess conceptual understanding through integration with prior frameworks and complementary transformations such as interpreting, exemplifying, classifying, and summarizing.
- **P173** — In unit review, compare intended wording, enacted activities, assessment content, and scoring criteria, using specific knowledge subtypes and processes to resolve conflicting signals.
- **P179** — Write course-specific goals as concrete statements of what students will be able to do after the course, with at least one goal under each taxonomy category the course claims, rather than leaving.
- **P182** — Operationalize cognitive categories with definitions, examples, assessment tasks, and instructional activities, and use disputed classifications to improve design rather than to win a labeling.
- **P184** — Classify a task for a specified learner group and prior-experience state.
- **P188** — Analyze intellectual performance at its actual transfer level.

## Assessment Design And Authentic Tasks

Skill: `assessment-design-and-authentic-tasks`

- **P012** — Match the assessment procedure to the kind of learning.
- **P016** — Anchor assessment in authentic tasks.
- **P017** — Require explicit criteria for evaluation, using internal consistency for checking and external standards for critique, and identify strengths and weaknesses before judgment.
- **P018** — Write each standard on a rubric scale as a description of what performance at that level looks like rather than as a bare numeric label, and derive the criteria from the verbs in the learning goal.
- **P021** — Protect criterion-based outcome judgement from administrative constraints.
- **P044** — Accept that beyond foundational knowledge and application the available assessment procedures are valid but less consistently reliable, and separate what is assessed for feedback from what is graded.
- **P047** — Distinguish exercising from forward-looking assessment.
- **P048** — Structure a reflective portfolio with explicit prompts.
- **P067** — Expect evidence of understanding to be less direct and more complicated than objective-test evidence.
- **P081** — Distinguish task grades from outcome achievement.
- **P084** — Maintain an alignment matrix from subordinate skills to condition-response-criterion objectives and parallel assessments, using authentic integrated products and explicit component rubrics.
- **P089** — Separate formative and summative purposes, label them unmistakably, and keep diagnostic practice safe enough for candid errors while using its evidence to change both learner and teacher action.
- **P097** — Design assessment with outcomes and learning activities, using direct or authentic performance and a varied programme of tasks only where each contributes distinct valid evidence.
- **P098** — Use a standards model for attainment.
- **P110** — Set pass and mastery standards from minimum acceptable qualitative performance, make ordered rubrics public, calibrate assessors on whole examples, and allow bounded reassessment when mastery rather.
- **P112** — Assess creativity in the disciplinary work through grounded originality, ambition, experiment, critical inquiry, contextual appropriateness, and effective realisation while keeping criteria open.
- **P114** — Design portfolios around strategic evidence selection, early guidance, bounded workload, open but relevant artefacts, and holistic outcome-profile judgement rather than item averaging.
- **P127** — If an examination is retained, design it for integrated understanding, prepare learners for efficient resource use, and supplement convergent questions with routes for relevant divergent evidence.
- **P128** — For consequential assessment, independently align objectives, taught substance, and assessed substance on both knowledge and process, while using only limited format practice to remove irrelevant.
- **P129** — Keep internal traceability in design records while presenting learner-facing aligned assessments without unnecessary codes, and use whole-task and subordinate-skill pretests to support fair branching.
- **P132** — Manage external assessment as a high-stakes constraint by mapping its underlying knowledge and processes against local objectives, teaching the substantive learning, limiting format rehearsal.
- **P167** — Design the assessment programme as an evidence system.
- **P199** — Build assessment as a scrapbook rather than a single snapshot.

## Feedback And Formative Practice

Skill: `feedback-and-formative-practice`

- **P022** — Plan for adjustment in light of goals, feedback, and predictable problems.
- **P033** — Give students opportunities to rehearse, consult resources, get feedback, and refine, and run learning as cycles of performance, feedback, revision, and new performance; the secure secret test.
- **P057** — Make feedback inform the learner of the degree of correctness of their performance; its delivery may be built into the medium, learner-initiated and immediate, or supplied later by an instructor.
- **P113** — Use shared quality criteria for instructor, peer, and self-review, collecting successive authentic products and adapting checklists as learners' judgment develops.
- **P130** — Make formative feedback timely, two-way, and gap-closing.
- **P165** — Repair relevance by grounding examples and practice in the authentic goal, use only fair taught distinctions in distractors, and provide immediate self-checkable feedback during embedded assessment.

## Teaching For Understanding And Transfer

Skill: `teaching-for-understanding-and-transfer`

- **P023** — Recognize that no additional fact tells the learner which fact to use when.
- **P034** — Integrate teaching of metacognitive skills across subject areas and make the processes explicit.
- **P045** — Teach comprehension monitoring as the capability of setting learning goals, estimating success against them, and selecting alternative strategies to meet them.
- **P056** — Do not count a task as a transfer problem if it differs from classroom examples only in quantities or symbols; it is new only if the student must modify the problem statement, cast it into a model.
- **P058** — Do not read a call for active inquiry and application around a big idea as a blanket endorsement of discovery learning.
- **P078** — Develop durable understanding by connecting new learning to prior knowledge, organising cases under higher-order relations, balancing guidance with learner construction, and revisiting ideas through.
- **P116** — Teach categories through their defining common structure and useful disciplinary applications; when a misconception is systematic, diagnose and repair the underlying categorization rather than adding.
- **P120** — Expect content standards to vary widely in form.
- **P131** — Teach strategy use conditionally.
- **P144** — Preserve warranted uncertainty in evidence-based judgment.
- **P154** — Treat big ideas as the meaningful patterns that connect the dots of otherwise fragmented knowledge and as the building material of understandings, and as linchpins without which students are left.
- **P163** — Provide access to intellectually substantial material through conceptual bridges, paraphrase, examples, performance, and structured comparison rather than lowering expectations solely from prior.
- **P171** — Teach self-regulation as both knowledge and action.
- **P174** — Design for meaningful learning by combining retained relevant knowledge with opportunities to construct understanding, solve problems, and use learning in new situations.
- **P196** — Evaluate instruction by transfer rather than memory.
- **P197** — Recognize the four senses of essential.
- **P198** — Review predictable misconceptions before teaching by asking what misinformation learners harbour and what rough spots always crop up.

## Multimedia And E-Learning Design

Skill: `multimedia-and-elearning-design`

- **P002** — Diagnose the cause before prescribing a fix.
- **P007** — Design against the three assumptions of the cognitive theory of multimedia learning.
- **P015** — Design for a schematic pictorial model, not a photographic copy.
- **P026** — Reject the search for a universally best medium.
- **P038** — Design for a small working memory.
- **P040** — Treat the appeal to individual learning styles as resting on a phrase whose specific meaning is not entirely clear.
- **P042** — Check learner prior knowledge before applying a design principle.
- **P092** — Require both criteria of a design principle before recommending it.
- **P093** — Do not add seductive details.
- **P124** — Match graphic type to what is being taught.
- **P155** — Set practice quantity from task criticality and the automaticity requirement, weighing learner time.
- **P156** — Analyse a multimedia message by how the learner processes it.
- **P157** — In a system-paced presentation, route words away from the visual channel when a graphic is present.
- **P158** — Treat the design problem as guiding appropriate cognitive processing without exceeding working-memory capacity, and remember that the three demands.
- **P159** — Contiguity principle 1.
- **P160** — Eliminate split attention wherever it appears in the interface.

## Instructional Strategy And Events

Skill: `instructional-strategy-and-events`

- **P005** — Add prerequisite instruction for demonstrated deficits, move broadly mastered capabilities into entry checks, and branch learners to only the teaching or review their profiles warrant.
- **P011** — Recognize that rules for teaching mental operations are conditional rather than absolute.
- **P019** — Prompt each example with attention-focusing information linking its specific attributes to the labels the definition uses, then fade that prompting in the later stages.
- **P024** — Have the component concepts and rules recalled just before the new learning.
- **P025** — Plan instruction deliberately at nested levels.
- **P027** — Require a defined concept to be demonstrated rather than stated.
- **P028** — Apply the sequencing principle proper to each outcome type.
- **P029** — Sequence like a zoom lens.
- **P032** — Introduce a concept only after learners have experienced the phenomenon it names, and open the section with a question arising from what they have just observed, so the definition labels something.
- **P035** — Replace an abstract statement of objectives with a concrete demonstration of the terminal capability the learner will acquire.
- **P036** — Employ repetition as spaced occasions for the learner to recall what was learned rather than as mere re-presentation.
- **P037** — Teach a concrete concept by presenting instances that vary widely in their non-relevant characteristics together with negative instances, so correct performance is shown by both selecting members.
- **P039** — Treat the nine events of instruction as a design checklist to be considered for any desired learning, not a mandatory script.
- **P051** — Practise against the stimulus named in the objective.
- **P052** — Make every practice and test instance new.
- **P053** — Delineate the distinctive features of every stimulus presented.
- **P054** — Sequence simple-to-complex within a hierarchy, teaching subordinate paths before the superordinate ones that contain them, while recognizing that the order between independent hierarchies is not.
- **P059** — Build deliberate recall of previously learned content into the lesson.
- **P060** — Follow the design procedure appropriate to each learner characteristic.
- **P063** — Sequence lessons by prerequisites and manageable progression, then use focused entry checks, authentic whole-task and lesson pretests, embedded feedback assessments, and component-plus-integrated.
- **P074** — Teach intellectual skills through activated prerequisites, explicit prior-to-new links, diagnostic examples and nonexamples, application steps and quality criteria, progressive transfer cases.
- **P086** — Scaffold generative performance from transforming supplied examples to independent creation, support execution with concise criteria, and show multiple representative solutions when several answers.
- **P117** — Derive instructional strategy from the goal, analyses, objectives, and assessments, covering motivation, presentation, examples, active practice, feedback, assessment, transfer, and delivery.
- **P118** — Evaluate instructional strategy across preinstruction, presentation, participation, assessment, follow-through, motivation, and operational management, adapting criteria to outcomes and learners.
- **P122** — Prescribe methods together with the situations they fit.
- **P123** — Apply encoding specificity and transfer-appropriate interaction.
- **P143** — Build fluency from conceptual relationships and progressively faded representations, prioritizing meaning before formal labels while preserving labels needed for later communication.
- **P146** — Use current motivation, outcome-type, and strategy principles as explicit design-analysis criteria, recording both strengths and weaknesses for defensible synthesis.
- **P147** — Teach classification with coherent learner-relevant examples, diagnostic nonexamples, removal of irrelevant shortcut cues, and rule-based explanations for every judgment.
- **P150** — Teach and assess multiattribute concepts with closely matched examples and nonexamples, immediate error review, and new but strategy-parallel items across pretest, practice, and posttest.
- **P180** — Budget time for motivation, readiness checks, instruction, practice, integration, and assessment, combining individualized delivery with prepared human support where learner readiness varies.
- **P181** — Develop strategy flexibility by having learners apply, explain, and compare multiple valid methods, and diagnose actual method use rather than inferring it from answers or speed alone.
- **P185** — Scaffold a complex procedure from reduced common material to independent projects, modeling both actions and decisions, monitoring guided practice, and remediating before release.
- **P189** — Teach verbal information in a relevant, prior-knowledge-linked organization and rehearse meaningful recall cues, using logical mnemonics only when meaningful elaboration is insufficient.
- **P190** — Influence attitudes by making desired and undesired choices and consequences explicit, using relatable credible models, and ensuring feedback delivers the promised consequence directly or vicariously.

## Motivation And Learner Engagement

Skill: `motivation-and-learner-engagement`

- **P030** — Set the challenge from accurate information about what these students can and cannot do at the start, so it stretches them a bit but not too far, and always pair a challenging activity.
- **P049** — Organise motivational design under attention, relevance, confidence, and satisfaction.
- **P080** — Build motivation by connecting intended learning to goals learners value and by making success credible, rather than manufacturing urgency through threatening assessment.
- **P100** — Read the standard cluster of student symptoms.
- **P133** — Treat deep and surface approaches as task-and-context dependent.
- **P169** — Combine autonomy and trust with clear goals, organised support, and fair safeguards; avoid intimidation, arbitrary control, or cynical busy-work that redirects attention toward compliance or escape.

## Needs And Context Analysis

Skill: `needs-and-context-analysis`

- **P050** — For complex tasks, teach the actual thought processes an expert uses while performing, not merely the task's outputs, and use objective methods to uncover them because experts are usually not.
- **P065** — Verify technology, access, reliability, context support, and instructor responsibilities from actual conditions, while triangulating learner characteristics and using authentic motivation suited.
- **P070** — Test adoption fit against current organizational goals and job needs, participant characteristics, and total acquisition, implementation, maintenance, facility, and equipment demands.
- **P075** — Operationalize a vague goal by eliciting observable success behaviors from several knowledgeable contributors, selecting the best indicators, and checking that they jointly preserve the goal's intent.
- **P079** — Split broad goals where subperformances have different structures, teach prerequisites when demonstrated entry performance is weak despite prior exposure, and describe learners, learning context.
- **P095** — When goal steps are unclear, draft the goal assessment and triangulate its required actions, cognition, and decisions through self-performance, multiple competent performers, workplace evidence, job.
- **P102** — Ground a behaviorally expressed instructional goal in a demonstrated need, then analyze the goal, prerequisites, learners, and use environments before writing condition-and-criterion performance.
- **P148** — Begin performance improvement without presuming instruction, and use proportionate front-end analysis to distinguish capability deficits from accountability, incentive, tool, process, and culture.
- **P187** — Conduct front-end performance analysis without presuming training; commit to needs assessment and instructional design only for verified skill gaps.
- **P191** — Trace a voiced problem to an organizational outcome, quantify desired-versus-actual result and job-performance gaps, separate controllable factors, and document causal evidence and candidate.
- **P192** — Diagnose performance with critical distance, multiple perspectives, direct evidence, contrary evidence, and a measurable chain from organizational outcomes through job standards to controllable.
- **P193** — Give a qualified content expert validated goals and skill frameworks as explicit review standards, converting them into structured coverage criteria rather than requesting an unbounded opinion.

## Iterative Prototyping And Development

Skill: `iterative-prototyping-and-development`

- **P010** — Choose representative samples of objectives within each outcome domain at intermediate planning levels rather than listing complete performance objectives everywhere, while recognising the fuller.
- **P068** — Design instruction as a dynamic system whose interdependent people, materials, activities, delivery mechanisms, and environments are monitored and revised from outcome feedback.
- **P073** — Use progressive release gates.
- **P085** — Converge as evidence stabilizes, resist perfection and unplanned refinements, release a solution fit for responsible use with correction capacity, and reopen approved design only for unacceptable.
- **P087** — Involve recent and representative learners while fundamental changes remain affordable; let them experience rough prototypes authentically, explain incompleteness only as needed, and shift review.
- **P119** — Begin and end every cycle with evaluation; keep the first cycle to a thin slice connecting performance goals, behavioral objectives, appraisal, practical media, representative treatments.
- **P151** — Estimate cost and duration from backgrounding, representative prototypes, treatment coverage, and committed resources; when funding is inadequate, reduce to a coherent effective core or phase.
- **P152** — Use experiential prototypes rather than specification-only approval to align stakeholder expectations and test timing, transitions, media, interaction, and integration before misunderstanding reaches.
- **P195** — Map objectives to treatments, reuse treatments where context and criteria permit, draft only enough objectives to prototype representative cases initially, and prototype each distinct.

## Evaluation Transfer And Impact

Skill: `evaluation-transfer-and-impact`

- **P041** — Expect an innovation to persist only when it has an identifiable support group and constituency and can be monitored cost-effectively; several prominent instructional innovations failed to persist.
- **P062** — Measure workplace use separately for each learned capability by degree, frequency, context, and prior use, and diagnose nonuse across relevance, learning, permission, support, resources.
- **P066** — Tie organizational impact to concrete use of learned capability, investigating adoption perceptions and social exchange while triangulating objective indicators, relevant observers, records.
- **P069** — Interpret transfer as the interaction of instructional relevance and practice, learner ability and motivation, and workplace opportunity, feedback, accountability, support, and incentives.
- **P071** — Combine achievement with structured and open learner reactions, investigate reported ambiguity, and map each diagnosed issue to an evidence-supported revision.
- **P072** — Screen instruction first for congruence with organizational needs, content completeness against validated analysis, and strategy fit to outcomes and transfer before funding an impact study.
- **P082** — Triangulate impact evidence across learners and relevant workplace observers, time measurement to the work and skill, and sample the differences most likely to affect implementation.
- **P091** — Compare interventions only after each is mature enough for routine use, center summative judgment on resolution of the originating problem, and use an independent evaluator when stakeholder interests.
- **P094** — Distinguish learner assessment from instructional evaluation, using formative assessment to improve learning, staged formative evaluation to revise development, and independent summative evaluation.
- **P096** — Evaluate impact only after target learners can perform in context, gathering unobtrusive workplace evidence about need resolution, capability use, and performance change, then report it as decision.
- **P099** — Frame impact questions from organizational, job, and learner outcomes; build trust and access, explain that training rather than participants is under evaluation, and tailor methods to each site.
- **P104** — Gate expert judgment through congruence, content, design, and transfer analyses, stopping when a material mismatch or uncorrectable content defect makes downstream evaluation unjustified.
- **P106** — Analyze learner-by-item-by-objective performance across pretest, rehearsal, and posttest, separating learners by entry readiness and verifying delayed retention when guided practice may mask missing.
- **P109** — Establish with valid posttest evidence that learners acquired every main goal performance before interpreting absent workplace behavior as a transfer failure.
- **P140** — Formatively evaluate a usable draft through one-to-one, small-group, and field evidence, tracing learner difficulty back to any implicated design assumption or component and revising accordingly.
- **P145** — Review every goal step and subordinate skill separately for accuracy, completeness, job alignment, and assessment coverage, stopping at material inaccuracy and preserving both coverage rates.
- **P149** — Test transfer feasibility by checking job aids and guidance, worksite-compatible technology, realistic simulation of critical conditions, and compatible equipment, supervision, and environments.
- **P178** — Trace each impact question to the evidence and instrument it requires, anchor measures in learned skills and job context, and prefer direct question-organized analysis unless the design requires.
- **P194** — Report purpose, questions, design, procedures, findings, recommendations, and evidence-based rationale, leading with an executive summary and stating limits introduced by scope compromises.

## Active Learning And Group Formats

Skill: `active-learning-and-group-formats`

- **P061** — Build authentic project environments with clear process scaffolds, examples, policies, job aids, purposeful teams, and the full specialist and administrative support ecosystem.
- **P083** — Launch authentic projects through a credible example and learner participation, investigate audience and product possibilities before fixing the plan, expose team plans to stakeholder critique.
- **P103** — Structure group learning so members have relevant resources, responsibility, psychological room to test interpretations, and a cognitively demanding agenda tied to assessed outcomes.
- **P108** — For large classes, choose rapid formats that still elicit intended structure or functioning, and use group evidence only with visible contribution, whole-task understanding, individual reflection.
- **P126** — In large classes, establish stable peer support, gather rapid formative evidence, and embed work-along or note-revision activity so learners' reasoning becomes visible.
- **P136** — Design peer-teaching programmes around explicit outcomes and roles, select and train suitable leaders, support them continuously, and use their feedback to improve the live course.
- **P137** — Educate professionals for informed action by combining a sound knowledge base with repeated authentic performance of the outcome before final assessment.
- **P138** — Teach creativity through sufficient disciplinary knowledge, open-ended creation, iterative performance and reflection, exploratory consequences, peer feedback, and a climate safe for unusual.
- **P139** — Design PBL from its intended outcomes.
- **P164** — Before grading learner choice among project options, calibrate options for cognitive demand, workload, required knowledge, support, and scoring, and explain those differences before selection.
- **P166** — Treat broad formats as settings, select different learner activities for different outcomes, and use lectures selectively for distinctive live value rather than routine information transfer.
- **P168** — For written or oral creation, interpret the full product demand, introduce purpose, audience, content, organization, and criteria early, and explicitly teach any rubric learners cannot yet use.
- **P175** — Align the whole PBL system.
- **P176** — Make practica reproduce professional behaviours and standards, normalise observation and formative feedback before summative judgement, and combine trustworthy evidence sources under shared outcomes.
- **P177** — Teach research as distinct judgments and procedures.
- **P183** — Embed authentic complex performance in a coherent context, coordinate its component knowledge and processes, and share functional product criteria before learners begin.
- **P186** — Gate drafting and creation on verified adequate, relevant, and accessible source material, then deliberately shift from enabling research procedures to the culminating product.

## Teaching Scholarship And Quality

Skill: `teaching-scholarship-and-quality`

- **P004** — Ground any claim that instruction is adequate in evidence about what students actually learn and how much they grow, not in demand, enrolment, or satisfaction.
- **P006** — Prefer replicated results synthesized across studies.
- **P043** — Treat instructional design as professional work requiring knowledge and practice, and use contrasts between weak and strong instruction to align leaders who underestimate that expertise.
- **P090** — Analyze realistic teaching cases as provisional evidence.
- **P107** — Make the teaching theory shaping the learning environment explicit, then use evidence and a coherent framework to diagnose problems and adapt responses to local learners and constraints.
- **P121** — Name the teacher's blind spot.
- **P134** — Improve teaching through repeated, systematic action-research cycles grounded in disciplinary knowledge, learning theory, local evidence, and triangulation across students and trusted colleagues.
- **P162** — Investigate teaching through an iterative evidence cycle.
- **P200** — Choose approaches by what learning needs rather than what teaching finds comfortable, expecting the right proportions to be a ratio one is not in the habit of using, and knowing each preference's.
