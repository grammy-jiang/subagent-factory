# Instructional-Design Principles Index

Package-wide index of every promoted principle, grouped by the skill that owns it. Each entry restates the principle's operative core; the full statement lives in `../principles/principles.yaml`.

## Backward Design And Constructive Alignment

Skill: `backward-design-and-constructive-alignment`

- **P006** — Keep the unit as the design focus.
- **P010** — Ensure students understand what the learning eventually requires them to do.
- **P013** — Understand backward design as called backward only because many teachers begin with the means, and follow its order.
- **P019** — Plan instruction deliberately at nested levels.
- **P041** — Expect the design process to be always iterative, with designers revisiting what they are after, how to assess it, and how to teach it, and rethinking earlier units in light of later designs.
- **P047** — Adapt constructive alignment to local context while preserving its core relationship.
- **P074** — Derive course and programme outcomes from graduate purposes, embedding both foundational and higher-order capabilities in authentic disciplinary or professional contexts.
- **P086** — Develop complex graduate capabilities through disciplinary practice, repeated programme experiences, institutional ethos, and integrated evidence.
- **P095** — Make evaluation part of the aligned course system.
- **P096** — When an activity stands in for learning, state the intended result and success criteria, connect them repeatedly to the activity, and show representative evidence.
- **P107** — Keep programme and course outcomes few, integrated, relevant, and manageable; each must state what learners will newly do and how well, while grouping compatible topics under common performances.
- **P116** — Prioritise content deliberately.
- **P153** — Implement alignment as one agenda.
- **P160** — Write course-specific goals as concrete statements of what students will be able to do after the course, with at least one goal under each taxonomy category the course claims, rather than leaving.

## Learning Outcomes And Taxonomy

Skill: `learning-outcomes-and-taxonomy`

- **P011** — Choose representative samples of objectives within each outcome domain at intermediate planning levels rather than listing complete performance objectives everywhere, while recognising the fuller.
- **P038** — Begin writing any objective by deciding what type of learning outcome the instruction aims to produce.
- **P059** — Set stage-appropriate levels of understanding and use SOLO to distinguish relevant detail from integration, coherent relation, and transfer to broader domains.
- **P060** — Resolve broad objective language into the specific knowledge subtype and cognitive process that successful learner performance actually requires.
- **P067** — Use taxonomy as an adaptable analytic heuristic, not a literal reality, source of educational aims, guaranteed solution, or unique teaching prescription.
- **P089** — Use analyze, evaluate, and create either as explicit transfer objectives or as means to simpler learning, and allow them to initiate or iterate with other processes rather than imposing a fixed.
- **P100** — Classify objectives from enacted learning demands and scoring criteria as well as wording, especially when standards or broad verbs conceal procedural application.
- **P105** — Abandon mastery for attitude objectives and measure strength instead as the proportion of times the person behaves in a given way across a sample of defined situations, framing the objective.
- **P123** — Distinguish routine execution from adaptive implementation; for the latter, require recognition of the problem type, selection or construction of a fitting method, adaptation, and execution.
- **P124** — Use analysis to select relevant elements, organize their relation to the whole, and infer underlying purpose; represent the resulting structure explicitly when useful.
- **P142** — To assess transfer or cognition beyond remembering, use sufficiently novel material to prevent memorized reproduction without adding irrelevant knowledge demands.
- **P151** — Build and assess conceptual understanding through integration with prior frameworks and complementary transformations such as interpreting, exemplifying, classifying, and summarizing.
- **P154** — In unit review, compare intended wording, enacted activities, assessment content, and scoring criteria, using specific knowledge subtypes and processes to resolve conflicting signals.
- **P163** — Operationalize cognitive categories with definitions, examples, assessment tasks, and instructional activities, and use disputed classifications to improve design rather than to win a labeling.
- **P165** — Classify a task for a specified learner group and prior-experience state.
- **P169** — Analyze intellectual performance at its actual transfer level.

## Assessment Design And Authentic Tasks

Skill: `assessment-design-and-authentic-tasks`

- **P014** — Anchor assessment in authentic tasks.
- **P015** — Require explicit criteria for evaluation, using internal consistency for checking and external standards for critique, and identify strengths and weaknesses before judgment.
- **P016** — Write assessment tasks forward-looking rather than backward-looking.
- **P018** — Ask of validity whether the test measures what it purports to, whether results correlate with other valid results, whether the sample represents what was taught, and whether results predict future.
- **P026** — Use the objective to set up the assessment situation directly.
- **P032** — Accept that beyond foundational knowledge and application the available assessment procedures are valid but less consistently reliable, and separate what is assessed for feedback from what is graded.
- **P035** — Distinguish exercising from forward-looking assessment.
- **P036** — Structure a reflective portfolio with explicit prompts.
- **P039** — Prefer criterion-referenced grading against predefined standards using performances and portfolios, with continual assessment of instruction, over norm-referenced grading on a curve judged.
- **P065** — Distinguish task grades from outcome achievement.
- **P069** — Maintain an alignment matrix from subordinate skills to condition-response-criterion objectives and parallel assessments, using authentic integrated products and explicit component rubrics.
- **P071** — Expect evidence of understanding to be less direct and more complicated than objective-test evidence.
- **P075** — Separate formative and summative purposes, label them unmistakably, and keep diagnostic practice safe enough for candid errors while using its evidence to change both learner and teacher action.
- **P082** — Design assessment with outcomes and learning activities, using direct or authentic performance and a varied programme of tasks only where each contributes distinct valid evidence.
- **P083** — Use a standards model for attainment.
- **P094** — Set pass and mastery standards from minimum acceptable qualitative performance, make ordered rubrics public, calibrate assessors on whole examples, and allow bounded reassessment when mastery rather.
- **P097** — Assess creativity in the disciplinary work through grounded originality, ambition, experiment, critical inquiry, contextual appropriateness, and effective realisation while keeping criteria open.
- **P099** — Design portfolios around strategic evidence selection, early guidance, bounded workload, open but relevant artefacts, and holistic outcome-profile judgement rather than item averaging.
- **P109** — If an examination is retained, design it for integrated understanding, prepare learners for efficient resource use, and supplement convergent questions with routes for relevant divergent evidence.
- **P110** — For consequential assessment, independently align objectives, taught substance, and assessed substance on both knowledge and process, while using only limited format practice to remove irrelevant.
- **P111** — Keep internal traceability in design records while presenting learner-facing aligned assessments without unnecessary codes, and use whole-task and subordinate-skill pretests to support fair branching.
- **P114** — Manage external assessment as a high-stakes constraint by mapping its underlying knowledge and processes against local objectives, teaching the substantive learning, limiting format rehearsal.
- **P136** — Build assessment as a scrapbook rather than a single snapshot.
- **P145** — Before grading learner choice among project options, calibrate options for cognitive demand, workload, required knowledge, support, and scoring, and explain those differences before selection.
- **P148** — Design the assessment programme as an evidence system.

## Feedback And Formative Practice

Skill: `feedback-and-formative-practice`

- **P017** — Plan for adjustment in light of goals, feedback, and predictable problems.
- **P024** — Use cheap recurring checks.
- **P027** — Give students opportunities to rehearse, consult resources, get feedback, and refine, and run learning as cycles of performance, feedback, revision, and new performance; the secure secret test.
- **P040** — Prefer anonymous or semi-anonymous immediate response over a show of hands.
- **P098** — Use shared quality criteria for instructor, peer, and self-review, collecting successive authentic products and adapting checklists as learners' judgment develops.
- **P112** — Make formative feedback timely, two-way, and gap-closing.
- **P146** — Repair relevance by grounding examples and practice in the authentic goal, use only fair taught distinctions in distractors, and provide immediate self-checkable feedback during embedded assessment.

## Teaching For Understanding And Transfer

Skill: `teaching-for-understanding-and-transfer`

- **P003** — Evaluate meaningful learning with transfer tasks that require prediction, diagnosis, mechanism explanation, or redesign in a new situation; use retention as a complementary measure rather.
- **P028** — Integrate teaching of metacognitive skills across subject areas and make the processes explicit.
- **P033** — Teach comprehension monitoring as the capability of setting learning goals, estimating success against them, and selecting alternative strategies to meet them.
- **P042** — Do not count a task as a transfer problem if it differs from classroom examples only in quantities or symbols; it is new only if the student must modify the problem statement, cast it into a model.
- **P061** — Develop durable understanding by connecting new learning to prior knowledge, organising cases under higher-order relations, balancing guidance with learner construction, and revisiting ideas through.
- **P104** — Name the teacher's blind spot.
- **P113** — Teach strategy use conditionally.
- **P126** — Preserve warranted uncertainty in evidence-based judgment.
- **P135** — Treat big ideas as the meaningful patterns that connect the dots of otherwise fragmented knowledge and as the building material of understandings, and as linchpins without which students are left.
- **P152** — Teach self-regulation as both knowledge and action.
- **P155** — Design for meaningful learning by combining retained relevant knowledge with opportunities to construct understanding, solve problems, and use learning in new situations.
- **P162** — Develop strategy flexibility by having learners apply, explain, and compare multiple valid methods, and diagnose actual method use rather than inferring it from answers or speed alone.
- **P176** — Recognize the four senses of essential.
- **P177** — Stay vigilant because teachers are prone to read correct answers as understanding while students want to appear to get it; that a few students get it and no one asks questions does not mean the rest.
- **P178** — Review predictable misconceptions before teaching by asking what misinformation learners harbour and what rough spots always crop up.
- **P179** — Treat the test of understanding as appropriate application of concepts and principles to newly posed questions rather than repetition of learned information or performance of mastered practices.

## Multimedia And E-Learning Design

Skill: `multimedia-and-elearning-design`

- **P001** — When animation and explanatory words must be processed together, prefer spoken narration to concurrent on-screen prose so verbal and pictorial processing are distributed across channels.
- **P004** — Do not duplicate narrated animation with concurrent on-screen sentences merely to add a delivery mode or satisfy presumed learning-style preferences.
- **P005** — Treat the high behavioral engagement of games and simulations as no evidence of learning; the design problem is securing the psychological activity.
- **P009** — Choose pedagogy before technology.
- **P020** — Signal the features that should drive selective perception.
- **P025** — Provide or activate an organizing structure or mental model for the new knowledge.
- **P043** — Do not design around learning styles.
- **P078** — Keep multimedia explanations coherent by removing irrelevant words, decorative images or video, and background sounds or music, even when those additions seem interesting or entertaining.
- **P134** — Design explanations to help learners construct coherent causal models by selecting relevant words and images, organizing each representation, and integrating those representations with one another.
- **P137** — Set practice quantity from task criticality and the automaticity requirement, weighing learner time.
- **P138** — For novices learning an unfamiliar system, provide self-paced pretraining on each essential component's name, location, and behavior before presenting the causal interaction of the whole system.
- **P139** — Contiguity principle 1.
- **P140** — Eliminate split attention wherever it appears in the interface.
- **P141** — Apply these principles as guidance consistent with how the mind works rather than as unbending rules, and state the boundary conditions whenever you invoke one.

## Instructional Strategy And Events

Skill: `instructional-strategy-and-events`

- **P008** — Add prerequisite instruction for demonstrated deficits, move broadly mastered capabilities into entry checks, and branch learners to only the teaching or review their profiles warrant.
- **P029** — Employ repetition as spaced occasions for the learner to recall what was learned rather than as mere re-presentation.
- **P030** — Teach a concrete concept by presenting instances that vary widely in their non-relevant characteristics together with negative instances, so correct performance is shown by both selecting members.
- **P037** — Choose the strategy by the information-processing stage it supports.
- **P046** — Sequence lessons by prerequisites and manageable progression, then use focused entry checks, authentic whole-task and lesson pretests, embedded feedback assessments, and component-plus-integrated.
- **P057** — Teach intellectual skills through activated prerequisites, explicit prior-to-new links, diagnostic examples and nonexamples, application steps and quality criteria, progressive transfer cases.
- **P072** — Scaffold generative performance from transforming supplied examples to independent creation, support execution with concise criteria, and show multiple representative solutions when several answers.
- **P101** — Teach categories through their defining common structure and useful disciplinary applications; when a misconception is systematic, diagnose and repair the underlying categorization rather than adding.
- **P102** — Derive instructional strategy from the goal, analyses, objectives, and assessments, covering motivation, presentation, examples, active practice, feedback, assessment, transfer, and delivery.
- **P103** — Evaluate instructional strategy across preinstruction, presentation, participation, assessment, follow-through, motivation, and operational management, adapting criteria to outcomes and learners.
- **P106** — Make training conditions resemble the conditions of retrieval.
- **P119** — Teach creativity through sufficient disciplinary knowledge, open-ended creation, iterative performance and reflection, exploratory consequences, peer feedback, and a climate safe for unusual.
- **P125** — Build fluency from conceptual relationships and progressively faded representations, prioritizing meaning before formal labels while preserving labels needed for later communication.
- **P129** — Teach classification with coherent learner-relevant examples, diagnostic nonexamples, removal of irrelevant shortcut cues, and rule-based explanations for every judgment.
- **P132** — Teach and assess multiattribute concepts with closely matched examples and nonexamples, immediate error review, and new but strategy-parallel items across pretest, practice, and posttest.
- **P144** — Provide access to intellectually substantial material through conceptual bridges, paraphrase, examples, performance, and structured comparison rather than lowering expectations solely from prior.
- **P149** — For written or oral creation, interpret the full product demand, introduce purpose, audience, content, organization, and criteria early, and explicitly teach any rubric learners cannot yet use.
- **P158** — Teach research as distinct judgments and procedures.
- **P161** — Budget time for motivation, readiness checks, instruction, practice, integration, and assessment, combining individualized delivery with prepared human support where learner readiness varies.
- **P166** — Scaffold a complex procedure from reduced common material to independent projects, modeling both actions and decisions, monitoring guided practice, and remediating before release.
- **P167** — Gate drafting and creation on verified adequate, relevant, and accessible source material, then deliberately shift from enabling research procedures to the culminating product.
- **P170** — Teach verbal information in a relevant, prior-knowledge-linked organization and rehearse meaningful recall cues, using logical mnemonics only when meaningful elaboration is insufficient.
- **P171** — Influence attitudes by making desired and undesired choices and consequences explicit, using relatable credible models, and ensuring feedback delivers the promised consequence directly or vicariously.
- **P172** — Teach motor and psychomotor skills through visual demonstration of the movement sequence and its timing, part-to-whole integration for complex performance, and repeated physical practice.

## Motivation And Learner Engagement

Skill: `motivation-and-learner-engagement`

- **P012** — Organise motivational design under attention, relevance, confidence, and satisfaction.
- **P021** — Set the challenge from accurate information about what these learners can and cannot do, so it stretches them a bit but not too far, adjust the difficulty as their progress shows, always pair.
- **P063** — Build motivation by connecting intended learning to goals learners value and by making success credible, rather than manufacturing urgency through threatening assessment.
- **P085** — Read the standard cluster of student symptoms.
- **P115** — Treat deep and surface approaches as task-and-context dependent.
- **P150** — Combine autonomy and trust with clear goals, organised support, and fair safeguards; avoid intimidation, arbitrary control, or cynical busy-work that redirects attention toward compliance or escape.

## Needs And Context Analysis

Skill: `needs-and-context-analysis`

- **P002** — Derive training needs from a documented discrepancy.
- **P022** — Assess the operating learning environment and prior performance-improvement system.
- **P048** — Verify technology, access, reliability, context support, and instructor responsibilities from actual conditions, while triangulating learner characteristics and using authentic motivation suited.
- **P052** — Test adoption fit against current organizational goals and job needs, participant characteristics, and total acquisition, implementation, maintenance, facility, and equipment demands.
- **P054** — Trace a voiced problem to an organizational outcome, quantify desired-versus-actual result and job-performance gaps, separate controllable factors, and document causal evidence and candidate.
- **P058** — Operationalize a vague goal by eliciting observable success behaviors from several knowledgeable contributors, selecting the best indicators, and checking that they jointly preserve the goal's intent.
- **P062** — Split broad goals where subperformances have different structures, teach prerequisites when demonstrated entry performance is weak despite prior exposure, and describe learners, learning context.
- **P080** — When goal steps are unclear, draft the goal assessment and triangulate its required actions, cognition, and decisions through self-performance, multiple competent performers, workplace evidence, job.
- **P127** — Review every goal step and subordinate skill separately for accuracy, completeness, job alignment, and assessment coverage, stopping at material inaccuracy and preserving both coverage rates.
- **P130** — Begin performance improvement without presuming instruction.
- **P168** — Conduct front-end performance analysis without presuming training; commit to needs assessment and instructional design only for verified skill gaps.
- **P173** — Give a qualified content expert validated goals and skill frameworks as explicit review standards, converting them into structured coverage criteria rather than requesting an unbounded opinion.

## Iterative Prototyping And Development

Skill: `iterative-prototyping-and-development`

- **P023** — Set goals through early design-prototype-evaluation cycles, then stabilize behavioral objectives that specify observable performance, its conditions, and success criteria before detailed design.
- **P050** — Design instruction as a dynamic system whose interdependent people, materials, activities, delivery mechanisms, and environments are monitored and revised from outcome feedback.
- **P056** — Use progressive release gates.
- **P070** — Converge as evidence stabilizes, resist perfection and unplanned refinements, release a solution fit for responsible use with correction capacity, and reopen approved design only for unacceptable.
- **P073** — Involve recent and representative learners while fundamental changes remain affordable; let them experience rough prototypes authentically, explain incompleteness only as needed, and shift review.
- **P133** — Use experiential prototypes rather than specification-only approval to align stakeholder expectations and test timing, transitions, media, interaction, and integration before misunderstanding reaches.
- **P175** — Map objectives to treatments, reuse treatments where context and criteria permit, draft only enough objectives to prototype representative cases initially, and prototype each distinct.

## Evaluation, Transfer And Impact

Skill: `evaluation-transfer-and-impact`

- **P045** — Measure workplace use separately for each learned capability by degree, frequency, context, and prior use, and diagnose nonuse across relevance, learning, permission, support, resources.
- **P049** — Tie organizational impact to concrete use of learned capability, investigating adoption perceptions and social exchange while triangulating objective indicators, relevant observers, records.
- **P051** — Interpret transfer as the interaction of instructional relevance and practice, learner ability and motivation, and workplace opportunity, feedback, accountability, support, and incentives.
- **P053** — Combine achievement with structured and open learner reactions, investigate reported ambiguity, and map each diagnosed issue to an evidence-supported revision.
- **P055** — Screen instruction first for congruence with organizational needs, content completeness against validated analysis, and strategy fit to outcomes and transfer before funding an impact study.
- **P066** — Triangulate impact evidence across learners and relevant workplace observers, time measurement to the work and skill, and sample the differences most likely to affect implementation.
- **P077** — Compare interventions only after each is mature enough for routine use, center summative judgment on resolution of the originating problem, and use an independent evaluator when stakeholder interests.
- **P079** — Distinguish learner assessment from instructional evaluation, using formative assessment to improve learning, staged formative evaluation to revise development, and independent summative evaluation.
- **P081** — Evaluate impact only after target learners can perform in context, gathering unobtrusive workplace evidence about need resolution, capability use, and performance change, then report it as decision.
- **P084** — Frame impact questions from organizational, job, and learner outcomes; build trust and access, explain that training rather than participants is under evaluation, and tailor methods to each site.
- **P088** — Gate expert judgment through congruence, content, design, and transfer analyses, stopping when a material mismatch or uncorrectable content defect makes downstream evaluation unjustified.
- **P090** — Analyze learner-by-item-by-objective performance across pretest, rehearsal, and posttest, separating learners by entry readiness and verifying delayed retention when guided practice may mask missing.
- **P093** — Establish with valid posttest evidence that learners acquired every main goal performance before interpreting absent workplace behavior as a transfer failure.
- **P122** — Formatively evaluate a usable draft through one-to-one, small-group, and field evidence, tracing learner difficulty back to any implicated design assumption or component and revising accordingly.
- **P128** — Use current motivation, outcome-type, and strategy principles as explicit design-analysis criteria, recording both strengths and weaknesses for defensible synthesis.
- **P131** — Test transfer feasibility by checking job aids and guidance, worksite-compatible technology, realistic simulation of critical conditions, and compatible equipment, supervision, and environments.
- **P159** — Trace each impact question to the evidence and instrument it requires, anchor measures in learned skills and job context, and prefer direct question-organized analysis unless the design requires.
- **P174** — Report purpose, questions, design, procedures, findings, recommendations, and evidence-based rationale, leading with an executive summary and stating limits introduced by scope compromises.

## Active Learning And Group Formats

Skill: `active-learning-and-group-formats`

- **P034** — Treat mastering the content as chiefly a means to the second task of learning to use it, and move initial content learning out of class because that is what frees the time the second task requires.
- **P044** — Build authentic project environments with clear process scaffolds, examples, policies, job aids, purposeful teams, and the full specialist and administrative support ecosystem.
- **P068** — Launch authentic projects through a credible example and learner participation, investigate audience and product possibilities before fixing the plan, expose team plans to stakeholder critique.
- **P087** — Structure group learning so members have relevant resources, responsibility, psychological room to test interpretations, and a cognitively demanding agenda tied to assessed outcomes.
- **P092** — For large classes, choose rapid formats that still elicit intended structure or functioning, and use group evidence only with visible contribution, whole-task understanding, individual reflection.
- **P108** — In large classes, establish stable peer support, gather rapid formative evidence, and embed work-along or note-revision activity so learners' reasoning becomes visible.
- **P117** — Design peer-teaching programmes around explicit outcomes and roles, select and train suitable leaders, support them continuously, and use their feedback to improve the live course.
- **P118** — Educate professionals for informed action by combining a sound knowledge base with repeated authentic performance of the outcome before final assessment.
- **P120** — Design PBL from its intended outcomes.
- **P147** — Treat broad formats as settings, select different learner activities for different outcomes, and use lectures selectively for distinctive live value rather than routine information transfer.
- **P156** — Align the whole PBL system.
- **P157** — Make practica reproduce professional behaviours and standards, normalise observation and formative feedback before summative judgement, and combine trustworthy evidence sources under shared outcomes.
- **P164** — Embed authentic complex performance in a coherent context, coordinate its component knowledge and processes, and share functional product criteria before learners begin.

## Teaching Scholarship And Quality

Skill: `teaching-scholarship-and-quality`

- **P007** — Ground any claim that instruction is adequate in evidence about what students actually learn and how much they grow, not in demand, enrolment, or satisfaction.
- **P031** — Treat instructional design as professional work requiring knowledge and practice, and use contrasts between weak and strong instruction to align leaders who underestimate that expertise.
- **P064** — Improve teaching through repeated, systematic action-research cycles grounded in disciplinary knowledge, learning theory, local evidence, and triangulation across students and trusted colleagues.
- **P076** — Analyze realistic teaching cases as provisional evidence.
- **P091** — Make the teaching theory shaping the learning environment explicit, then use evidence and a coherent framework to diagnose problems and adapt responses to local learners and constraints.
- **P121** — Make leadership an enabling system for department-wide alignment.
- **P143** — Investigate teaching through an iterative evidence cycle.
- **P180** — Choose approaches by what learning needs rather than what teaching finds comfortable, expecting the right proportions to be a ratio one is not in the habit of using, and knowing each preference's.
