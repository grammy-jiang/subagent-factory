# Professional Practice And Revision

## Purpose

This skill reviews the professional workflow that keeps a translation usable. It follows an explicit procedure from source to target and the client's style guide; revises another translator's work by finding and fixing genuine errors rather than imposing a personal style; routes queries and spotted errors to the client rather than footnoting confusion; handles auto-generated tables of contents and strict space constraints correctly; and allows non-mother-tongue work only with native-language revision.

## When to use

- A revision is rewriting another translator's text as the reviser's own rather than fixing genuine errors.
- Confusion or a spotted error is being footnoted instead of raised with the client, or a style guide is being ignored.
- An auto-generated table of contents or a hard space constraint (leaflet, string limit, label) is mishandled.
- Non-mother-tongue translation is being done without qualified native-language revision or stylistic advice.

## Procedure

1. Comply with the client's style guide governing grammar, sentence structure, terminology, punctuation, address, headings, and product names (P079).
2. Allow non-mother-tongue translation only with appropriate competence and controls such as qualified native-language revision or stylistic advice when target-native expectations matter (P087).
3. When revising another translator's work, find and fix errors rather than rewrite it as your own (P111).
4. Never use footnotes in a professional translation to flag confusion or queries (P112).
5. On spotting an apparent error, notify the client and choose (P113).
6. Do not translate an auto-generated table of contents first or in place (P114).
7. Meet strict space constraints (P115).
8. Follow an explicit procedure from source to target (P116).

## Inputs

- The translation workflow under review — the procedure, style guide, revision, queries, or constraints in play.
- The reasoning offered for the decision under review: the audience, brief, strategy, and any naturalness or quality claim made.

## Output

Per finding: name the flaw and the principle it violates, apply the correction, state the residual uncertainty and the trade-off it reflects, and end with a concrete next step. Order findings highest-impact first. This skill reviews a translation, a translation choice, or a translation-studies analysis; it does not produce the finished translation or make the publication decision.

## Anti-patterns to flag

- The analysis fails to comply with the client's style guide governing grammar, sentence structure, terminology, punctuation, address, headings, and product names (P079).
- The analysis fails to allow non-mother-tongue translation only with appropriate competence and controls such as qualified native-language revision or stylistic advice when target-native (P087).
- The analysis fails to when revising another translator's work, find and fix errors rather than rewrite it as your own (P111).
- The analysis fails to never use footnotes in a professional translation to flag confusion or queries (P112).
- The analysis fails to on spotting an apparent error, notify the client and choose (P113).
- The analysis fails to do not translate an auto-generated table of contents first or in place (P114).
- The analysis fails to meet strict space constraints (P115).
