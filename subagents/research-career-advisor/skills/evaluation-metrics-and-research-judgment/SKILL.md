# Evaluation Metrics And Research Judgment

## Purpose

This skill guides the evaluation and durable judgment a research career turns on — how a
research group generates ideas, how lab and publication metrics are read, how research
trainees are screened and ranked, and how expertise travels into a new subfield — so each
judgment stays honest about its assumptions, gameability, and scope.

## When to use

- A research group is generating candidate problems or solutions that must be preserved and
  independently attacked before an existing or known solution is revealed (P001).
- A researcher's scientific or systems judgment cannot be reduced to classroom rules and must
  be built through technically grounded practice, varied cases, and timely critique — e.g.
  mentoring a trainee or auditing your own read of a result (P018).
- Research students or staff are being trained or prepared for tools, methods, and conditions
  that will change across a research career, not just for today's test or technique (P028).
- A lab, department, or funder metric — citations, paper counts, an h-index, an activity tally
  — will drive research rewards, status, funding, or selection and must be tested for
  relevance, gameability, and time-horizon effects, favouring delivered scientific outcome
  quality (P029).
- PhD or postdoc applicants (or award candidates) are screened long before the advanced
  research role they are being selected for, so the screen must be checked against
  later-needed research capabilities, not only entry-stage traits (P030).
- A researcher's expertise is applied to a broader, unfamiliar, or changed subfield or problem
  and its hidden assumptions and scope must be surfaced (P036).
- Local or specialized research work is being justified, and its connection to delivered
  scientific value for the wider group and stakeholders must be traced rather than an activity
  tally (P037).
- Applicants or researchers must be ranked or ordered for a scarce research position, award,
  or limited slot (P038).

## Procedure

1. When ideas or candidate solutions are being generated, keep the group brainstorming without
   immediate judgment, preserve every candidate raised, and hold back any existing or known
   solution until participants have independently attacked the problem themselves (P001).
2. When judgment is being built or assessed, check that it develops through technically
   grounded practice, exposure to varied cases, mixed-experience work alongside more and less
   experienced practitioners, and critique delivered promptly enough to preserve the real
   context of the event — not only through classroom rules (P018).
3. When people are being trained or evaluated for a career that will outlast today's tools,
   check that the plan cultivates learning-to-learn, durable fundamentals, and integrated
   competence, and that it exercises the ability to formulate new problems — not just pass
   isolated, fixed-format tests — because the conditions and tools in use at selection time
   will differ from those needed later (P028).
4. When a metric drives rewards, status, or decisions, treat it as an intervention: test it
   for relevance to the outcome it claims to measure, test whether it can be gamed, simulate
   how participants will adapt their behavior once the metric is known, project its effect
   across the relevant time horizon, and prefer outcome quality over an inflatable production
   count (P029).
5. When a selection or evaluation process screens people long before the role they are being
   screened for, check that the traits assessed line up with the capabilities that role will
   actually need — precise execution, synthesis, and prudent experimentation — rather than
   filtering candidates through only entry-stage traits such as test scores or credentials at
   intake (P030).
6. When expert judgment is carried into a broader, unfamiliar, or changed problem, surface the
   assumptions hidden inside that expertise, state its scope explicitly, and prefer recognized
   uncertainty to false understanding (P036).
7. When local or specialized work is being justified or reported, connect it to valuable
   delivered system behavior, widening the stakeholder boundary and time horizon considered
   rather than optimizing for activity volume or systems rhetoric (P037).
8. When people must be ranked for scarce roles, make the reduction to a scale explicit,
   deliberate, and reviewable — showing how the score was built — rather than presenting it as
   if it captures the whole person (P038).

## Inputs

- The metric, selection process, ranking, brainstorming session, or judgment call under
  review, the decision it drives, and the assumptions and time horizon behind it.
- The reasoning offered for the decision under review: the goal, the plan or practice in
  place, and any claim of importance, readiness, or soundness made.

## Output

Per finding: name the gap and the principle it engages, give the correction, state the
residual trade-off or the referral to make, and end with a concrete next step. Order findings
highest-impact first. This skill advises on research-career and empirical practice; it does
not run the study, write the paper or grant, or make the hiring, admission, funding, or tenure
decision for the caller.

## Anti-patterns to flag

- A facilitator screens out or vetoes ideas as they surface, or reveals an existing solution
  before participants have independently attempted the problem, so the candidate set narrows
  prematurely and only the facilitator's favored answer survives (P001).
- Training consists only of decontextualized classroom rules or a single repeated case,
  mixed-experience pairing is avoided, and critique arrives long after the event with the
  original context stripped away, so tacit judgment never develops (P018).
- A curriculum or training plan optimizes for passing today's isolated tests and current
  tools rather than durable fundamentals or learning-to-learn, so trainees cannot reformulate
  problems or adapt once conditions and tools change (P028).
- A metric is adopted and tied to rewards or status without ever being tested for relevance
  or gameability, and a raw, inflatable production count stands in for outcome quality (P029).
- A selection process filters candidates on entry-stage traits alone — test scores or
  credentials at intake — without assessing the precise execution, synthesis, or prudent
  experimentation that the later, advanced role actually requires (P030).
- An expert applies a rule or heuristic to a broader or changed problem without surfacing the
  assumptions behind it or stating its scope, presenting false certainty instead of the
  recognized uncertainty the situation calls for (P036).
- A status report or justification cites local activity volume or systems rhetoric — tickets
  closed, hours logged — without tracing that work to any delivered value for the wider set of
  stakeholders or a longer time horizon (P037).
- A ranking or averaged score is presented as if it captures the whole person, with no
  visibility into how the reduction to that scale was made and no way to review it (P038).
