# Research-Career & Method Principles Index

Package-wide index of every promoted principle, grouped by the skill that owns it. Each entry restates the principle's operative core; the full statement lives in `../principles/principles.yaml`.

## Writing And Publishing Scientific Work

Skill: `writing-and-publishing-scientific-work`

- **P003** — Before submission, expose every premise and logical link, distinguish assumptions from established results, and disclose known weaknesses rather than strengthening claims beyond the evidence.
- **P006** — Make a paper's significance discoverable at every entry point with an accurate title, a brief results-focused abstract, and an introduction that explains the question, contribution, and change.
- **P008** — Preserve reproducibility without sacrificing readability by publishing the methodological detail needed for independent evaluation and separating optional specialist depth from the main argument.
- **P009** — Obtain demanding local review before submission, including language editing when needed, and treat a referee's misunderstanding as a prompt to improve exposition unless it reveals a substantive.
- **P012** — Prefer a small portfolio of high-quality, influential papers over fragmenting a strong contribution into many weak publications.
- **P013** — Build a doctoral dissertation as a coherent, logically ordered body of substantial advances around one explicit thesis, excluding unrelated or merely incremental work and supplying the problem, prior.
- **P032** — Make the abstract a self-contained account of problem, gap, contribution, evidence, impact, and material limitation, then expand those points in the same order in the introduction with background.
- **P046** — After finding a result, clean and explain its logic, then recast it into the broadest form genuinely supported without hiding scope or limitations.

## Presenting And Engaging With Research

Skill: `presenting-and-engaging-with-research`

- **P002** — Build a research talk as one rehearsed scientific story whose opening establishes the question, context, and importance at a depth appropriate to the audience, then connects methods and results.
- **P007** — Communicate important ideas through formal talks, written reports, and timely informal explanation; improve by critiquing real presentations and adapting effective techniques.
- **P027** — Use every slide to advance the scientific argument, omit filler, and include technical detail only when it is necessary for the intended audience to understand a novel method or result.
- **P039** — Read for research in layers.

## Choosing Advisers, Groups, And Positions

Skill: `choosing-advisers-groups-and-positions`

- **P010** — Prefer an established adviser when other mentoring qualities are comparable.
- **P011** — Research the prospective group, ask informed questions, and explain a genuine complement between the candidate's goals and the group's work; prepare the teaching contribution as well for faculty.
- **P021** — Select a postdoctoral position for the trainee's career needs.
- **P024** — Consider building a peer-recognized publication record in a research laboratory before seeking a senior academic role.
- **P033** — Evaluate an adviser through trainee evidence.
- **P034** — Investigate how a prospective laboratory manages priorities, resources, performance, collaboration, and internal mobility.

## Early-Career Positioning And Negotiation

Skill: `early-career-positioning-and-negotiation`

- **P004** — Use collaboration without surrendering research identity.
- **P016** — Research prospective colleagues and present evidence of initiative, intellectual leadership, collegiality, and a concrete first two-to-three-year agenda rather than unfocused breadth or willingness.
- **P019** — Align each main project's expected output with the appointment horizon; require a credible publication and support plan, pair long work with shorter results, and move groups if timely progress cannot.
- **P026** — Negotiate a result-oriented start-up package before committing.
- **P042** — Deliberately learn the professional skills that technical training omits, and cultivate an experienced mentor outside the direct authority chain for candid career advice.

## Research Program And Problem Selection

Skill: `research-program-and-problem-selection`

- **P015** — Orient a scientific program around consequential questions the researcher can explain and partly own; let those problems determine the techniques, learning capabilities central to independence.
- **P017** — Decompose a long research agenda into complete, distinct, publishable milestones, explain how each advances the larger goal, disseminate defensible advances promptly, and never pad the record.
- **P022** — Choose worthy, demanding goals, prepare steadily for chance opportunities, and revisit a future plan so daily choices and self-development accumulate toward the intended life.
- **P023** — Reserve recurring strategic time to audit field direction and maintain a portfolio of important problems, ranking them by credible growth and attackability and moving quickly when a clue changes.
- **P025** — Map important problems against methods, compare along both dimensions, and validate promising empty regions through sustained work that builds distinctive methodological command and a recognizable.
- **P035** — Begin significant work without waiting for ideal conditions, persist through expected uncertainty, and abandon the problem or attack when evidence shows that persistence has become a trap.
- **P043** — Maintain a manageable portfolio of projects so blocks, field decline, or lost publication priority in one do not halt output, while protecting rigor and the ability to finish.

## Funding, Grants, And Research Proposals

Skill: `funding-grants-and-research-proposals`

- **P005** — Build an early-career grant around important, achievable projects, treating a major long-term idea as exploratory and including enough advanced work to demonstrate results within the award cycle.
- **P020** — Search and credit related work, explain the proposal's distinct contribution, join credible group bids without surrendering needed evidence of individual initiative, and apply to every genuinely.
- **P031** — Assess impact through the work's ability to open a direction, support broad follow-on use, solve an important class of problems, or deliver a material advantage over established methods.
- **P044** — Judge creativity by useful value and meaningful recombination, not by novelty, execution difficulty, or immediate popularity alone.
- **P045** — Challenge an impossibility claim by enumerating its assumptions, testing their applicability, and obtaining another perspective when the frame may be narrow.

## Experimental Design And Measurement

Skill: `experimental-design-and-measurement`

- **P014** — Assume reported precision may be optimistic until the uncertainty process accounts for apparatus tuning and selection effects and is checked by independent, holdout, or later measurements.
- **P040** — For null-hypothesis testing, obtain an appropriate null sampling distribution, express the observed deviation in standard-error units when applicable, and reject only under a preselected unlikely.
- **P041** — Use a fully crossed factorial design only for factor combinations and interactions that answer the research claims; split the study when unneeded higher-order interactions would make the trial count.
- **P047** — Validate instruments, test apparatus, and self-generated telemetry independently before treating them as more trustworthy than the system they measure.
- **P048** — When intended service life exceeds the validation window, treat accelerated-test extrapolation as uncertain, plan validation explicitly, and build reliability into the design.

## Evaluation Metrics And Research Judgment

Skill: `evaluation-metrics-and-research-judgment`

- **P001** — Brainstorm without immediate judgment, preserve every candidate, and reveal existing solutions only after participants have independently attacked the problem.
- **P018** — Develop tacit professional and systems judgment through technically grounded practice, varied cases, mixed-experience work, and timely critique that preserves real context.
- **P028** — Prepare people for changing future conditions by cultivating learning-to-learn, durable fundamentals, integrated competence, and the ability to formulate problems beyond isolated tests.
- **P029** — Treat every evaluative metric as an intervention.
- **P030** — Align early selection and evaluation with capabilities needed later, retaining precise execution, synthesis, and prudent experimentation rather than filtering future leaders through only entry-stage.
- **P036** — Use expertise with explicit humility.
- **P037** — Connect local work to valuable delivered system behavior, widening the stakeholder boundary and time horizon rather than optimizing activity volume or systems rhetoric.
- **P038** — When people must be ranked for scarce roles, make the reduction to a scale explicit, deliberate, and reviewable rather than pretending the score captures the whole person.
