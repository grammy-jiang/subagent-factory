# Paper Sections And Organization

## Purpose

This skill guides the structure of a research paper and how each IMRaD section does its job. It checks that the introduction defines a concrete problem and funnels to a question calibrated to the audience and venue, that Methods are reproducibly complete, that Results are ordered top-down by interpretive dependency, that the Discussion reconnects to the goal and bounds its conclusions and limitations, and that the abstract, title, and conclusions are differentiated from the body rather than duplicating it. It builds each section from its rhetorical functions and checks the form against authentic target-journal examples.

## When to use

- An introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, abstract, conclusions, or title needs writing or reviewing for its section-specific job.
- A paper's overall organization must be built around the argument and the field's expected structure.
- Background, openings, or limitations need calibrating to the audience, venue, and section function.
- A reusable section model is being built from rhetorical functions and verified against field examples.

## Procedure

1. Make the introduction define a concrete problem and narrow to a question: never write that little is known, state specifically what is unknown, prefer a concrete small gap to a fuzzy one, and never sell a solution before defining the problem (P003).
2. Recast rather than copy overlapping Abstract content: let the Abstract compress topic, purpose, and findings while the Introduction establishes context and literature, and infer the local balance from matched target-journal examples (P004).
3. Whether you separate or combine Results and Discussion, ground the choice in making the reader's job easy and letting readers distinguish what you found from what you think; distinguish data, inference, and interpretation, and combine sections (often chronologically) when the field, data type, or sequential experiments warrant it (P005).
4. Organize a Results section top-down by orienting readers and giving an evidence-faithful overall pattern before individual findings, repeating an overview only when a new result set requires a distinct analytical frame (P025).
5. Calibrate background to audience and section function: establish only the context readers need, then move quickly through the limitation to the study, findings, and advance; a specialized venue may support a findings-first opening (P030).
6. Tailor the opening to the intended audience and venue, reframing the same core message with the characters and concerns that engage a specific journal or funding agency, because for generalist papers and proposals the opening can decide acceptance (P031).
7. Conclude the core interpretive argument with a reliable takeaway that connects findings to the original question and existing knowledge without exceeding supported populations, conditions, or outcomes (P035).
8. Distinguish reporting an observed trend from interpreting it through a model, and keep model-based or subjective interpretation out of standalone Results when the venue expects separation (P046).
9. Use active title wording, searchable nouns, and evidence-backed concrete differentiators; verify uncertain phrasing in a scholarly corpus and replace bare self-praise such as "novel" with the feature that is new (P048).
10. Choose a Methods opening from target-journal precedent, report completed work in past tense, use present for established methods, and select active or passive voice by focus without turning a research report into a directive protocol (P049).
11. Make Methods reproducibly complete by reporting applicable subjects, setting, design, assumptions, variables, treatment, precautions, equipment, collection, analysis, criteria, advances, and outcome-sensitive quantities, while using adequate citations for unchanged routine procedures (P050).
12. For a tool or apparatus Abstract, emphasize the capability, benefits, performance, and validation; for a problem-solution science Abstract, move from gap through solution and comparison to practical implication, keeping context normally under one quarter of the Abstract (P060).
13. Build a reusable section model from rhetorical functions, verify it against authentic field examples, learn the language that realizes each function, and use it as a scaffold rather than a permanent constraint (P061).
14. When a limitation affects interpretation, discuss it in the body of the Discussion — never the opening or resolution power positions — open with a methodological-considerations section only for unusual or novel methods, never make a limitation the conclusion, separate negatives and positives into linked arcs joined by a phrase like "despite these limitations," and address limitations honestly because doing so makes the work credible and citable (P062).
15. Structure Discussion around aim support, comparison, interpretations, limitations, validity, contribution, transfer, implications, and future research; follow applicable reporting standards and open by reconnecting to the goal or principal result before the immediate conclusion (P076).
16. Bound any external generalization with evidence and context, and close Discussion with supported takeaways, extensions, improvements, exclusions, implications, recommendations, or future work that complete the interpretive argument (P087).
17. Use the body to define concepts and hypotheses, supply accessible prerequisite background, develop reasoning, present central evidence, and establish the detailed conclusions promised earlier (P091).
18. For a broader audience or a diverse review panel, use a quick two-step opening that first engages the audience's interest and then narrows to your specific focus, keeping it to two steps and delaying your true central characters when the reviewers care more about an adjacent topic (P094).
19. Order findings by interpretive dependency and mark a contrasting, surprising, or especially important result set through paragraph structure and an accurate early relational cue (P095).
20. Use declarative, question, playful, or two-part titles only after checking recent venue practice, and ensure the form delivers a robust answer or distinct specification without sensationalism or repetition (P099).
21. After establishing the research space, identify the paper's purpose, focus, and organization, then preview only enough method and findings to define the contribution and connect those moves into a continuous progression (P101).
22. At the Discussion opening, recall only the background, methodological features, and principal findings needed to launch interpretation, without introducing overdue method detail or repeating Results at length (P106).
23. Use limitations to bound present conclusions and motivate specific, answerable future projects, balancing retained evidence without allowing positive findings to erase a material constraint (P110).
24. Differentiate Conclusions from the Abstract through informed synthesis and added implications, revise the two together for redundancy, and place paper-wide synthesis in Conclusions rather than duplicating it at the Discussion end (P117).
25. Replace assignment echoes, generic definitions, and inflated universal openings with community-specific context; use a fact, anecdote, or permitted quotation only when it anticipates key concepts and illuminates the problem (P119).
26. Report provenance and spatial configuration precisely enough to identify consequential materials, equipment, and component relationships, using figures to complement rather than replace essential prose (P127).
27. When using a prior method, name its source and characterize the present procedure accurately as reproduced, lightly adapted, or substantially changed, elevating only consequential modifications as contributions (P142).
28. Use the flexible Discussion model — backward reference, key-result summary, research mapping, contribution, implication, limitation, application, and future work — as a target-field-tested checklist rather than a mandatory sequence (P158).
29. Build the introduction body as a funnel from the opening's large problem to the challenge's specific question, framing the knowledge gap (which taps unexpectedness and emotion), and developing the logical connection at each step rather than telling everything you know (P159).

## Inputs

- The paper or section under discussion, its intended venue and audience, and the field's structural conventions.
- The reasoning offered for the decision under review: the goal, the audience and venue, the draft or plan in place, and any claim of clarity, rigor, or readiness made.

## Output

Per finding: name the gap and the principle it engages, give the correction, state the residual trade-off or the referral to make, and end with a concrete next step. Order findings highest-impact first. This skill advises on research writing and presentation; it does not write the paper, section, slides, or talk for the caller, guarantee acceptance or publication, or rule on the domain-science correctness of the research.

## Anti-patterns to flag

- Introduction claims "little is known" or leaves the gap vague and generic instead of naming a specific unresolved question, or pitches a solution before the problem is established (P003).
- Abstract and Introduction repeat the same sentences almost verbatim instead of the Abstract compressing findings and the Introduction supplying context and literature (P004).
- Results and Discussion blend raw data with the author's inference or opinion so readers cannot tell what was found from what is being argued (P005).
- Results section opens with isolated details or a table dump before any orienting overall pattern, forcing readers to assemble the picture themselves (P025).
- Background over-explains elementary material irrelevant to a specialist reader, or a specialist submission opens with lengthy review instead of moving quickly to findings (P030).
- The same generic opening is reused across venues and audiences without reframing the core message for what a specific journal's or agency's readers actually care about (P031).
- The Discussion's takeaway claims apply beyond the populations, conditions, or outcomes the data actually support (P035).
- Results slips into model-based or subjective interpretation of a trend in a venue that expects Results and Discussion kept separate (P046).
- Title uses passive or vague phrasing, unsearchable terms, or bare self-praise such as "novel" or "innovative" with no concrete differentiator behind it (P048).
- Methods opening ignores target-journal precedent, or reads as a step-by-step directive protocol instead of a report of completed work in the appropriate tense and voice (P049).
- Methods omits a reproducibility-critical element (subjects, equipment, criteria, or an outcome-sensitive quantity) or cites a routine procedure without adequate reference (P050).
- Abstract for a tool or apparatus buries capability and validation, or a problem-solution Abstract skips straight to implication without the gap-solution-comparison progression, or runs well past a quarter of the paper (P060).
- Prose imitates a rhetorical-function checklist so rigidly that natural variation across model papers is lost, or no target-field examples were checked before drafting (P061).
- A limitation appears only in the Discussion's opening or closing, is treated as the paper's conclusion, or is left unaddressed rather than honestly discussed in the body (P062).
- Discussion skips required functions such as comparison or limitations, or opens by restating method detail instead of reconnecting to the goal or principal result (P076).
- Discussion's closing claims a generalization unsupported by the evidence and context actually presented, or ends without a completing takeaway, implication, or future-work statement (P087).
- Body paragraphs jump straight to evidence without defining the concepts or hypotheses, or without the prerequisite background a qualified but non-expert reader needs (P091).
- Opening jumps immediately to the paper's true central characters and focus without first engaging a broad or mixed-interest review panel, or the engaging step runs past two moves (P094).
- Findings are ordered by narrative convenience rather than dependency, so a later result assumes one not yet introduced, or a surprising result set lacks an early relational cue (P095).
- Title uses a question, playful, or two-part form not standard for the venue, or the form fails to deliver a robust answer or specification and instead sensationalizes or repeats content (P099).
- Introduction jumps to method or findings detail before establishing purpose, focus, and organization, or previews so much detail that the progression toward the contribution stalls (P101).
- Discussion opening re-walks Results at length or introduces new method detail instead of recalling only what is needed to launch interpretation (P106).
- Limitations are stated but never tied to a specific, answerable future project, or a strong positive finding is used to wave away a material constraint (P110).
- Conclusions section merely restates the Abstract or duplicates the Discussion's closing synthesis instead of adding informed synthesis and new implications (P117).
- Introduction opens with an assignment echo, a generic dictionary-style definition, or an inflated universal claim instead of community-specific context (P119).
- Materials or equipment description is vague about provenance or spatial configuration, or a figure stands in for prose needed to identify a consequential relationship (P127).
- A method borrowed from prior work is presented without naming its source, or a routine reproduction is inflated into a claimed contribution (P142).
- Discussion is forced through every element of the flexible model in a fixed sequence, treating a target-field-tested checklist as a mandatory structure (P158).
- Introduction body reveals the whole picture at once, telling everything known, instead of funneling logically from the broad problem to the specific question and gap (P159).
