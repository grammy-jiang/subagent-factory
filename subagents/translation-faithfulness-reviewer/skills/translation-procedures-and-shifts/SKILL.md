# Translation Procedures And Shifts

## Purpose

This skill reviews micro-level translation decisions with the precise metalanguage of procedure and shift. It checks the Vinay & Darbelnet ladder (direct: borrowing, calque, literal; oblique: transposition, modulation, equivalence, adaptation) and reads Catford's level and category shifts not as the translator wishing to change the work but as functional fidelity, while watching the supplementary procedures for their hazards.

## When to use

- A micro-level rendering is being justified or criticised and needs naming with the procedure/shift metalanguage.
- A departure from formal correspondence needs classifying as a Catford level or category shift and read as functional, not arbitrary.
- A supplementary procedure (amplification, false friends, over-translation) may be distorting economy or meaning.
- A direct procedure is being applied where an oblique one is required for acceptability.

## Procedure

1. Work the Vinay and Darbelnet procedure ladder (P004).
2. Know Vinay & Darbelnet's four oblique procedures (P007).
3. Use the supplementary procedures and watch their hazards, weighing amplification against economy in word count, catching false friends that deceive, explicitating implicit information, and generalizing to a more general term, and (P053).
4. Analyze departures from formal correspondence as Catford's shifts, of two major types (P079).
5. Know Vinay & Darbelnet's three direct procedures and when each applies (P119).
6. Reframe shifts (Catford's deviations across linguistic levels) not as the translator wishing to change the work but as functional fidelity (P121).

## Inputs

- The source segment, its rendering, and the procedure/shift rationale offered.
- The reasoning offered for the decision under review: the orientation, strategy, brief, and any quality claim made.

## Output

Per finding: name the flaw and the principle it violates, apply the correction, state the residual uncertainty and the trade-off it reflects, and end with a concrete next step. Order findings highest-impact first. This skill reviews a translation, a translation choice, or a translation-studies analysis; it does not produce the finished translation or make the publication decision.

## Anti-patterns to flag

- The analysis fails to work the Vinay and Darbelnet procedure ladder (P004).
- The analysis fails to know Vinay & Darbelnet's four oblique procedures (P007).
- The analysis fails to use the supplementary procedures and watch their hazards, weighing amplification against economy in word count, catching false friends that deceive, explicitating (P053).
- The analysis fails to analyze departures from formal correspondence as Catford's shifts, of two major types (P079).
- The analysis fails to know Vinay & Darbelnet's three direct procedures and when each applies (P119).
- The analysis fails to reframe shifts (Catford's deviations across linguistic levels) not as the translator wishing to change the work but as functional fidelity (P121).
