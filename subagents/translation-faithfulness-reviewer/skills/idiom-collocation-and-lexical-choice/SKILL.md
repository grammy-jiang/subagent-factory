# Idiom, Collocation And Lexical Choice

## Purpose

This skill reviews the rendering of idioms, fixed expressions, and collocations, whose meaning cannot be deduced from their parts and whose target equivalent cannot be assumed. It reads a word through its collocational pattern rather than a dictionary gloss, applies idiom strategies by fit, and accepts that some loss, addition, or skewing is unavoidable when no exact collocational match exists.

## When to use

- A stretch of language may be an idiom or fixed expression and its rendering needs checking.
- A source word occurs in a fixed combination and is being read in isolation rather than through its collocation.
- An idiom is assumed to have a target equivalent when the way a language expresses a meaning is unpredictable.
- An unmarked collocation with no exact target match is being rendered and the trade-off of typicality against accuracy is unexamined.

## Procedure

1. Treat idioms and fixed expressions as frozen, largely invariant patterns whose meaning, for idioms, cannot be deduced from their parts, and make recognising the idiom the first step, using the cue that the less literal sense an (P042).
2. Apply idiom strategies by fit (P043).
3. At the interpretation stage, read a word through its collocational pattern rather than substituting a dictionary equivalent (P106).
4. Translate by paraphrase (P141).
5. Balance typicality against accuracy when rendering a collocation, accepting that some loss, addition, or skewing is unavoidable and judging any change by its significance in context; favour established target patterns that keep the (P142).

## Inputs

- The idiom, fixed expression, or collocation under review and its proposed rendering.
- The reasoning offered for the decision under review: the orientation, strategy, brief, and any quality claim made.

## Output

Per finding: name the flaw and the principle it violates, apply the correction, state the residual uncertainty and the trade-off it reflects, and end with a concrete next step. Order findings highest-impact first. This skill reviews a translation, a translation choice, or a translation-studies analysis; it does not produce the finished translation or make the publication decision.

## Anti-patterns to flag

- The analysis fails to treat idioms and fixed expressions as frozen, largely invariant patterns whose meaning, for idioms, cannot be deduced from their parts, and make recognising the idiom (P042).
- The analysis fails to apply idiom strategies by fit (P043).
- The analysis fails to at the interpretation stage, read a word through its collocational pattern rather than substituting a dictionary equivalent (P106).
- The analysis fails to translate by paraphrase (P141).
- The analysis fails to balance typicality against accuracy when rendering a collocation, accepting that some loss, addition, or skewing is unavoidable and judging any change by its (P142).
