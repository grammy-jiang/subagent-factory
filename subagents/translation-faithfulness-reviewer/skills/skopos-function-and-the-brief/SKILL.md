# Skopos, Function And The Brief

## Purpose

This skill reviews whether a translation is driven by an explicit brief and by the target's intended function. It checks the skopos rules in hierarchical order — purpose first, then intratextual coherence, then fidelity — establishes whether the job is an instrumental or a documentary translation, and orients the text toward its set of addressees and its medium even when that set is fuzzy.

## When to use

- A translation lacks an explicit brief specifying function, audience, medium, and conditions.
- Whether the job is instrumental (freestanding target) or documentary (a record of the source) is undecided.
- A skopos is being used to license neglect of stylistic or connotative detail.
- Audience and medium require adjusting, omitting, or making implicit meaning explicit.

## Procedure

1. Orient every translation toward a set of addressees even when that set is indeterminate and fuzzy (P002).
2. Establish whether the job is an instrumental translation (P027).
3. Apply the skopos rules in hierarchical order (P073).
4. Adjust for the audience and medium (P074).
5. Discover genre from everyday lingua-cultural practice, expect translation to remain in the source genre, and route null target-genre realizations to version or non-verbal alternatives when covert equivalence is desired (P139).

## Inputs

- The translation brief (or its absence), the source text type, and the target's intended function.
- The reasoning offered for the decision under review: the orientation, strategy, brief, and any quality claim made.

## Output

Per finding: name the flaw and the principle it violates, apply the correction, state the residual uncertainty and the trade-off it reflects, and end with a concrete next step. Order findings highest-impact first. This skill reviews a translation, a translation choice, or a translation-studies analysis; it does not produce the finished translation or make the publication decision.

## Anti-patterns to flag

- The analysis fails to orient every translation toward a set of addressees even when that set is indeterminate and fuzzy (P002).
- The analysis fails to establish whether the job is an instrumental translation (P027).
- The analysis fails to apply the skopos rules in hierarchical order (P073).
- The analysis fails to adjust for the audience and medium (P074).
- The analysis fails to discover genre from everyday lingua-cultural practice, expect translation to remain in the source genre, and route null target-genre realizations to version or (P139).
