# Translation Faithfulness Principles Index

Package-wide index of every promoted principle, grouped by the skill that owns it. Each entry restates the principle's operative core; the full statement lives in `../principles/principles.yaml`.

## Equivalence Theory And Orientation

Skill: `equivalence-theory-and-orientation`

- **P001** — Recognize that 1960s-70s equivalence theory is fundamentally normative.
- **P005** — Choose the equivalence orientation by purpose.
- **P009** — Understand dynamic equivalence.
- **P012** — Accept that because no two languages are identical in their symbols or arrangements there can be no absolute correspondence and hence no fully exact translation.
- **P022** — Weight content and form by their communicative function, preserving form more strongly when it carries genre, emotional force, or poetic effect.
- **P029** — In dynamic equivalence, make formal adjustments where literary form, idioms, or culturally embedded emotive meanings would otherwise block or distort receptor understanding.
- **P040** — Treat equivalence as approximate value across differences, assessed at text level and prioritized case by case across denotative, connotative, pragmatic, text-normative, and formal-aesthetic demands.
- **P058** — Use dynamic-equivalence strategies when the target task is to reproduce the receptor effect or relationship to the message rather than to display source form.
- **P059** — Evaluate translation adequacy by sense, source spirit and manner, natural receptor expression, and similar audience response, while accepting multiple valid solutions.
- **P061** — Assess meaning preservation across semantic, pragmatic, and textual dimensions rather than reducing equivalence to referential content.
- **P072** — Because equivalent effect can be illusory when a text is out of target space and time, follow Newmark in choosing method by text type.
- **P107** — Choose which level of equivalence to prioritize by text type.
- **P113** — Analyse equivalence at multiple levels.
- **P118** — Pitch equivalence at the level of the overall impression the message makes, not element-by-element identity.
- **P122** — Understand formal equivalence.
- **P123** — Hold a good translation to the synthesized requirements.
- **P125** — Take Reiss's frame: interlingual translation is bilingual mediated communication aiming at a target text functionally equivalent to the source, with the translator as a secondary sender, so some.

## Word And Grammar-Level Equivalence

Skill: `word-and-grammar-level-equivalence`

- **P006** — Reject cross-code identity as the basis of translation.
- **P014** — Diagnose the specific type of word-level non-equivalence.
- **P041** — Distinguish the propositional, expressive, presupposed, and evoked meanings of a word or utterance when assessing a rendering, recognising that a charge of inaccuracy usually concerns propositional.
- **P044** — Handle grammatical gender by its function.
- **P063** — Expect grammar to constrain the translation like a straitjacket.
- **P070** — Never assume a one-to-one correspondence between words and meanings across languages.
- **P085** — Always satisfy obligatory receptor-language features.
- **P095** — Do not assume an idiom has a target equivalent.
- **P096** — Do not map tense and aspect mechanically.
- **P099** — Do not equate sameness of referent with sameness of signification.
- **P112** — Resolve the categories the source and target languages obligatorily force.
- **P117** — Distrust dictionary equivalence.

## Technical Translation And Usability

Skill: `technical-translation-and-usability`

- **P015** — Let target-user needs govern additions, omissions, condensation, restructuring, and explicitness.
- **P017** — Balance fidelity, intelligibility, speed, cost, audience need, and target-culture acceptability instead of pursuing abstract perfection.
- **P018** — Use one-idea sentences and uniform phrasing to reduce memory load, build habits, improve recognition, and free attention for the task.
- **P019** — Apply iconic linkage by expressing recurring semantically identical information with the same target-language construction and preserving latent repetition.
- **P033** — Preserve user trust by making guides correct, confidence-building, and clearly oriented to customer needs.
- **P034** — Use repetition, sentence flow, and parallel structure only when they reinforce action, memory, clarity, or habit formation.
- **P048** — Make target-relevant information easy to find, select, assimilate, and proportionally redundant.
- **P049** — Combine literal translation, paraphrase, modification, addition, omission, and other strategies locally under one communicative purpose.
- **P062** — Use repetition and practice to support procedural automaticity without creating unnecessary dependence.
- **P068** — Prevent likely errors, make recovery easy, and use consistent familiar terminology and interaction patterns.
- **P093** — Do not assume consistency is disruptive; inconsistent instruction phrasing may be more harmful than repeated structures.
- **P094** — Scale consistency through controlled language, style guides, translation memory, and improved semantic-match tooling.
- **P104** — Make safety-critical information explicit, repeated where needed, and escalated to the client when the source is deficient.
- **P108** — Treat regulatory and normative documents.
- **P109** — For EU texts between official languages, consult EUR-Lex for the authoritative translations of terminology and directive names; a directive carries a short reference code.
- **P110** — Accommodate the client's mandated preferred terminology even when its rules.
- **P111** — Leave units of measure unchanged wherever possible, especially SI quantities.
- **P149** — Expect information conveyed as bare facts and specifications.

## Documentation Research And Empirical Method

Skill: `documentation-research-and-empirical-method`

- **P024** — Treat verbal reports, behavioural process measures, and neuroimaging as limited evidence that cannot by itself establish underlying cognition or translation quality.
- **P026** — Measure retention, task time, error rates, and satisfaction with explicit instruments and report the aggregate outcomes.
- **P035** — Avoid think-aloud protocols when representative task performance is the measurement target.
- **P050** — Prefer indirect overt recording when observer effects would distort performance, while preserving ethical recording practice.
- **P069** — In comparative documentation studies, isolate the manipulated variable and keep fonts, layout, graphics, content, and other confounds equivalent.
- **P105** — Define a small, observable, product-relevant set of performance criteria before testing.

## Quality Assessment And Error Analysis

Skill: `quality-assessment-and-error-analysis`

- **P003** — Decide whether a translation is overt.
- **P013** — For overt translation, aim at second-level access to the source function, preserve the source as intact as possible, and balance necessary explanation against replacement of source-culture markedness.
- **P016** — Build the quality comparison from a detailed source-text profile that specifies the function to be sought in the target text.
- **P030** — Use micro-, macro-, and superstructural levels to judge error seriousness only as part of a specified, text-context-sensitive procedure.
- **P031** — Treat preservation of factual content as insufficient when target choices weaken addressee involvement, flattery, reassurance, indirectness, responsibility management, hedging, or interpersonal force.
- **P055** — Evaluate a translation by comparing it against the source in the source language, giving priority to the elements where error silently distorts or reverses the argument.
- **P060** — Do not accept target-only, reception-only, or response-only evaluations as sufficient when they cannot explain the source-translation relation or distinguish translation from adaptation.
- **P078** — Judge translation quality by probabilistic, not deterministic, rules.
- **P088** — Allow analyst judgement only as argued, evidence-constrained hypotheses, acknowledging that equivalence and TQA retain non-absolute and subjective elements.
- **P089** — Classify unjustified cultural-filter changes as covert versions, and classify added special-audience or special-purpose renderings as overt versions rather than ordinary translations.
- **P092** — In Mode analysis of mission statements, distinguish oral-rhetorical writtenness from plain information delivery and evaluate repetition, parallelism, rhetorical macrostructure, and summative closure.
- **P135** — Classify overt errors separately as denotative omissions, additions, substitutions, wrong selections or combinations, ungrammaticality, and questionable acceptability.
- **P136** — State the assumptions and exceptions behind dimensional error judgements, including cultural comparability, intertranslatability, and whether the target has an added special function.
- **P137** — Weight errors by source-profile priorities, evaluation objective, and functional component, treating denotative mismatches as especially serious when ideational function is central.
- **P138** — Measure revised-model quality by analogous source and target profile/function analysis while distinguishing dimensional from non-dimensional mismatches.
- **P140** — Do not treat local cohesion as sufficient when translation loses source macrostructural parallelism, rhetorical guidance, oral-rhetorical effect, or summative closure.

## Culture, Poetics And Rewriting

Skill: `culture-poetics-and-rewriting`

- **P020** — Locate the deep problem of translating a foreign work not in the dictionary or semantic equivalence but in a compromise between two poetics in which the receiving system's poetics dominates.
- **P021** — Reject fluency ('reads as if originally written') as the measure of a good translation and reject reproduction of meaning as its aim.
- **P028** — Catch the subtractive changes that flatten a carefully constructed source text.
- **P037** — Read a translation in relation to the rival versions it reacts to, not in isolation.
- **P038** — Apply Borges's evaluative method.
- **P047** — Consider experimental and creative strategies for literary and philosophical texts, including Pound's energizing, archaizing, foreignizing style and use of a deliberately non-contemporary idiom to.
- **P057** — Read economic and institutional constraints as shaping which works are refracted and how.
- **P077** — Value a translation for its creative transformation and not fidelity alone.
- **P080** — Follow Frawley's argument that a respectable theory of translation must abandon the notions of good, bad, and fidelity just as it abandoned identity and 'preservation of meaning'.
- **P081** — Catch expansion and the two poles of stylistic distortion and impoverishment.
- **P082** — Catch the effacement of vernacular, idiom, and superimposed languages, and prefer literal translation to the Platonic figure.
- **P083** — Understand that this metaphorics encodes the relation between the value of production and reproduction with power consistently at stake.
- **P115** — Contest value-free descriptions and account for the value-driven institutional network of a translation, including the publishers and editors who choose, commission, pay, and dictate method plus.
- **P116** — Treat every translation choice as selective preservation.
- **P131** — Translate poetry as receptor-language poetry that elicits comparable feeling, rather than as word-for-word formal correspondence.
- **P132** — When music is preserved, allow metrical adaptation, theme rearrangement, omission, addition, or alteration.
