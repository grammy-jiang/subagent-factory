## [0.2.0] — 2026-06-10

### Changed

- **Full re-derive from primary source.** The profile was previously generated from an
  EssentialInsight third-party summary only. Version 0.2.0 rebuilds the profile against
  the full primary book: Chris Voss and Tahl Raz, *Never Split the Difference: Negotiating
  As If Your Life Depended On It* (HarperBusiness, 2016), ingested as
  `voss-chris-never-spl-20260610132145`. The summary is retained as a corroborating source
  only; it governs nothing the primary source addresses.

- **Tier 2 upgrade.** `tier: 2` added. Ten principles (PRIN-001–PRIN-010) were derived
  via the Tier-1 evidence chain from 64 extracted claims (CL001–CL064) across 64 evidence
  records (EV001–EV064). All `quality_bar` items, `forbidden_behaviours` items, mode
  `output` descriptions, and `knowledge_partition.always_on` items are now grounded in
  named principle IDs.

- **Agent version bumped** from 0.1.0 to 0.2.0.

- **`role`** rewritten to reference specific Voss techniques by name (mirroring, labeling,
  accusation audit, calibrated questions, Ackerman bargaining, Black Swan discovery) and to
  acknowledge the FBI/consulting provenance of the method.

- **`quality_bar`** updated to cite principle IDs (PRIN-001 through PRIN-010) as the
  grounding authority for each falsifiable check.

- **`forbidden_behaviours`** updated to cite principle IDs where applicable.

- **`knowledge_partition.always_on`** expanded from 3 items to 4 items; each item now
  cites the relevant principle IDs.

- **`source_of_truth_policy`** updated: canonical owner now names the primary book and
  source ID; two-source precedence rule (primary > summary > caller judgement) is explicit.

- **`sources`** block updated to list both sources; primary book listed first.

- **Two-source precedence rule** added to `source_of_truth_policy.precedence` and
  provenance ledger conflict log.

- **`tests/golden-tests.yaml`** rebuilt: test IDs GT-001, GT-002, GT-003 now match the
  `test_cases` references in `principles.yaml`; MC-001 (negative routing) added.

### Notes

- Rights on the primary book: no explicit open-licence notice; authored work →
  distillation-only floor applied. No verbatim passage reproduction in any generated
  artifact. Provenance ledger records this decision.
- No `produce` or `patch-suggest` modes: evidence gaps in interrogation records confirm
  the source does not model the expert drafting standalone deliverables or making bounded
  incremental patch suggestions. No `policy/patch-policy.yaml` generated.
- All skill and reference stubs from v0.1.0 are preserved unchanged; content expansion
  is deferred to the skills-authoring phase.
