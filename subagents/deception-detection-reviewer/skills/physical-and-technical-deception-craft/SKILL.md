# Physical And Technical Deception Craft

## Purpose

Audits whether the physical and technical construction behind a plant — a
staged sabotage, a corpse or courier carrying planted documents, a discovery
site, an exfiltration channel, a funding trail, or a claimed exaggeration of
strength — carries the signatures of a deliberately engineered deception
rather than the tells of an under-built one. Because a target can physically
examine what has been planted, weak craft here is directly falsifiable and
can unravel an otherwise sound deception; this skill is a lens for
recognising the adversary's toolkit in what has been found, not a set of
instructions for building one.

## When to use

- A reported explosion, fire, "accident," or act of sabotage sits in the case
  record and needs testing for the signature of a deliberate, controlled
  plant rather than a genuine hostile or accidental event.
- A corpse, courier, or dead-drop artefact anchors a claim, and the
  personality and documents built around it need checking for whether they
  are exhaustively real enough to be believed.
- Physical evidence reached the adversary, or reached the analyst, through a
  discovery, and the site and path by which it was found need testing for
  whether they were engineered rather than merely convenient.
- Material is crossing an exfiltration channel neither side fully controls —
  a diplomatic bag, a neutral's routine mail — and needs checking for whether
  that channel is being actively exploited or only passively tolerated.
- A quantitative claim — an exaggerated strength figure, or a fall-of-shot /
  battle-damage report shaping an aim correction — needs testing for whether
  it is disciplined and plausibly aimed, or an overreach that gives it away.
- A funding arrangement or a piece of adversary-supplied equipment sits in
  the record and needs tracing for exposure risk or recycled captured
  matériel.
- The case's mix of communication channels looks concentrated on one
  technology and needs checking against the alternatives it may have
  abandoned.

## Procedure

1. Test any reported sabotage, fire, or "accident" — incident or friendly
   cover-story draft — for the staged-plant signature: does it leave physical
   evidence that actually points to the claimed cause, is the investigator or
   press narrative consistently steered toward that explanation, and is it
   "too effective," destroying the very evidence needed to credit it (P018)?
2. Test any corpse-, dead-drop-, or courier-carried document trail for the
   documented-personality signature: is the man behind the papers built out
   with real, exhaustively consistent letters, tickets, and identity
   documents, since the documents are only believed if the man is (P041)?
3. Test the discovery of any physical artefact for the planting-site
   signature: is the site one the adversary could plausibly access yet a
   thorough forensic pass would struggle to fully unpick, and did more than
   one plausible path lead to its discovery (P085)?
4. Test any material crossing a channel neither side fully controls for
   active exploitation versus passive tolerance: is content deliberately
   seeded into it, and has a well-placed neutral courier's traffic been
   examined for secret writing piggybacked on legitimate correspondence
   (P037)?
5. Test any quantitative claim feeding an adversary's targeting or
   order-of-battle picture for calibrated bias: does a fall-of-shot or
   battle-damage record show an unexplained directional asymmetry consistent
   with steering a correction, and does an exaggerated strength claim hold
   to a disciplined bound on the order of ten percent (P044, P081)?
6. Trace the funding and equipment plumbing behind a controlled operation: do
   cross-currency payments route through a plausible commercial exchange that
   would not expose the arrangement, and has any adversary-supplied equipment
   been examined as possibly recycled captured matériel (P082, P084)?
7. Check the case's communication channel mix for over-concentration on one
   technology, since the contest between interception and evasion keeps
   shifting: has personal contact or secret writing been abandoned in favour
   of wireless alone, or the reverse (P046)?
8. Emit findings highest-impact first, per the output contract below.

## Inputs

- The case record or plan under review: any reported sabotage, fire, or
  accident, with what physical evidence survived and how investigators or
  the press explained it.
- Any planted document, courier, or corpse-carried personality, with the
  letters, tickets, and identity papers built around it.
- The discovery circumstances of any physical artefact: the site, who could
  plausibly access it, and the path or paths by which it was found.
- Any material known to be crossing an uncontrolled exfiltration channel —
  a diplomatic bag, a neutral courier — and what is known about its content
  and use.
- Quantitative claims feeding the adversary's picture: strength figures,
  fall-of-shot or battle-damage reports, and their apparent bias or bound.
- The funding arrangement behind the operation and any adversary-supplied
  equipment entered into the record.
- The case's mix of communication channels and technologies in use or
  proposed.

## Output

Per finding: name the flaw, apply the correction, state the residual
uncertainty the correction leaves — including who could still be deceiving
whom — and end with a concrete next step. Order findings highest-impact
first. Never issue a bare go/no-go on the plant or the case; hand the
corrected judgment and the residual risk back to the operation's owner.

Example: a reviewed sabotage report shows scorched debris but no surviving
mechanism consistent with the claimed cause (P018). The finding names this
as a possibly over-destroyed, staged plant, recommends re-engineering the
physical evidence so it would survive a forensic pass, and notes the
residual uncertainty is whether investigators — or the adversary — already
suspect the destruction was too convenient; the next step handed back is to
commission an independent forensic reconstruction before the story is used
operationally.

## Anti-patterns to flag

- A staged sabotage so clean or so destructive that it erases the very
  evidence needed to credit it, or an investigation or press narrative that
  does not match what the physical evidence actually supports (P018).
- A planted document trail resting on a corpse or courier whose personality
  is thin, generic, or inconsistent — documents asked to carry more belief
  than the man behind them can support (P041).
- A physical find whose discovery path is single, fragile, or oddly
  convenient, with no redundant route by which it could plausibly have been
  found (P085).
- An uncontrollable channel — diplomatic bag, neutral courier — left
  unexamined and unexploited: tolerated as a leak rather than checked for
  seeded content or secret writing riding on it (P037).
- A fall-of-shot or battle-damage record with an unexplained directional
  bias never checked for whether it is steering, or being steered by, an aim
  correction (P044).
- A strength claim stretched far beyond a disciplined bound on the order of
  ten percent, risking either implausibility or a misjudged credulity
  threshold (P081).
- A funding trail or adversary-issued equipment accepted at face value,
  never traced for exposure and never checked against the possibility of
  recycled captured matériel (P082, P084).
- A channel mix concentrated on a single communication technology, with
  older alternatives — personal contact, secret writing — abandoned rather
  than preserved as fallbacks (P046).
