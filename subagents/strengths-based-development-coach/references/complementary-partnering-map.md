# Complementary partnering map

How to manage a lesser-talent area and how to pair people so one person's dominant
theme covers another's gap. Use this whenever a weakness must be handled or a team
grid surfaces a coverage gap.

## The three managing strategies (apply in order)

When a lesser-talent area must be addressed, work down this sequence; only consider
deficit training after all three are genuinely exhausted, and then name its
high-cost / low-return trade-off (P-004).

| Order | Strategy | What it means | Example |
|-------|----------|---------------|---------|
| 1 | Avoidance | Eliminate or reduce exposure to the task where structurally possible (CL-024). | Restructure a role so a low-talent task moves off the person's plate. |
| 2 | Systems & workarounds | Build a tool or process that compensates for the gap (CL-025). | Use a day planner or electronic calendar to cover weak detail management. |
| 3 | Complementary partnering | Pair with a colleague whose dominant themes cover the gap (CL-026). | Hand the detail work to a partner whose Discipline or Arranger theme makes it effortless. |

Complementary partnering does more than patch a weakness: it can uncover hidden
talents and build a stronger team (CL-026).

## Partnering logic

A pairing is *complementary* when one person's gap maps onto another person's
dominant theme. To find pairs:

1. Name the gap as a domain of talent (e.g. detail/structure, people-influence,
   forward-vision, decisiveness).
2. Identify which of the partner's dominant themes naturally cover that domain.
3. Recommend the pair **by naming the specific themes** — not generic "find someone
   different" advice (P-009).

Illustrative domain → covering-theme groupings (derived from the theme essences in
`34-theme-reference-card.md`; treat as a starting map, not a fixed rule):

| Gap domain | Themes that tend to cover it |
|------------|------------------------------|
| Order, detail, follow-through | Discipline, Arranger, Responsibility, Focus |
| Influence, rapport, winning others over | Woo, Communication, Command, Activator |
| Forward vision, options, direction | Futuristic, Strategic, Ideation |
| Decisiveness, conviction under uncertainty | Self-Assurance, Command, Activator |
| Relationship depth, reading people | Relator, Empathy, Individualization, Developer |
| Analysis, evidence, rigour | Analytical, Deliberative, Context |

## Team-grid use (P-009)

In a team strengths grid, after mapping members × dominant themes:

- For each member's gap, scan other members' dominant themes for a cover and name the
  pairing (CL-036).
- Where a whole team lacks a domain, flag it as a team-level gap to manage with the
  three strategies above.
- Where every member shares a theme, flag the shared over-expression risk.
