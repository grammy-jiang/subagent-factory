# Skopos, Function And The Translation Brief

## Purpose

This skill reviews whether a translation strategy is driven by an explicit brief and the target text's intended function. It checks that the skopos is negotiated and specified before strategy, treats the skopos as the decisive factor that dethrones the source into one constituent of the commission, applies function plus loyalty so functionality never becomes a licence for anything, distinguishes documentary from instrumental translation, and refuses to let a fulfilled skopos excuse micro-level neglect of stylistic or semantic detail.

## When to use

- A translation lacks an explicit brief specifying purpose, audience, medium, and conditions.
- Whether the job is documentary (a record of the source) or instrumental (a freestanding target) is undecided.
- A skopos is being used to license neglect of stylistic or semantic detail, or is pitched against the author's intentions with no loyalty check.
- The target function is being read off the source profile instead of defined pragmatically from purpose and audience.

## Procedure

1. Treat translation as purpose-driven human interaction that guides cooperation across cultural barriers (P003).
2. Define target-text function pragmatically from purpose, audience, initiator, translator, and situation; do not infer it from the source text alone (P015).
3. Make the translation commission an explicit instruction stating both the goal and the conditions for attaining it (P038).
4. Apply function plus loyalty (P050).
5. Recognize that most texts are hybrids carrying several coexisting functions, that the target function may legitimately differ from the source function, and that the method depends on far more than text type (P051).
6. Let the commissioner's constraints set the macro-level characteristics and overall strategy while the translator decides the micro-level procedures, conditioning translator's notes on genre and skopos (P053).
7. Understand why mere trans-coding will not produce a serviceable translatum (P058).
8. Do not let a fulfilled skopos excuse micro-level neglect (P073).
9. Ground a reflective translation commentary in the translation problem exploration space of purpose or skopos, method, and readership to establish the target norms, capturing them in a translation specification that records the (P075).
10. Frame translation, with Vermeer, as a variety of purposeful translational action whose technical aim is the skopos, negotiated with the commissioning client (P112).
11. Let the purpose or skopos determine the translation (P145).
12. Apply functionality plus loyalty, treating functionality as the most important criterion but not a licence for anything (P146).
13. Take Holz-Manttari's translatorial action seriously (P149).
14. Let the skopos, not the source text or the source's text variety, determine the appropriate text variety of the translatum (P150).

## Inputs

- The translation brief (or its absence), the source text type, and the target's intended function.
- The reasoning offered for the decision under review: the orientation, strategy, brief, and any quality claim made.

## Output

Per finding: name the flaw and the principle it violates, apply the correction, state the residual uncertainty and the trade-off it reflects, and end with a concrete next step. Order findings highest-impact first. This skill reviews a translation, a translation choice, or a translation-studies analysis; it does not produce the finished translation or make the publication decision.

## Anti-patterns to flag

- The analysis fails to treat translation as purpose-driven human interaction that guides cooperation across cultural barriers (P003).
- The analysis fails to define target-text function pragmatically from purpose, audience, initiator, translator, and situation; do not infer it from the source text alone (P015).
- The analysis fails to make the translation commission an explicit instruction stating both the goal and the conditions for attaining it (P038).
- The analysis fails to apply function plus loyalty (P050).
- The analysis fails to recognize that most texts are hybrids carrying several coexisting functions, that the target function may legitimately differ from the source function, and that the (P051).
- The analysis fails to let the commissioner's constraints set the macro-level characteristics and overall strategy while the translator decides the micro-level procedures, conditioning (P053).
- The analysis fails to understand why mere trans-coding will not produce a serviceable translatum (P058).
